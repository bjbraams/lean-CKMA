## Weak derivatives and approximation

[Mathematical scope](SYNOPSIS26.md#weak-derivatives-and-approximation).

### Mathlib

On a finite-dimensional real normed space, for a measure finite on compact sets and \(1\leq p<\infty\), smooth compactly supported functions are dense in \(L^p\) (`MeasureTheory.Lp.dense_hasCompactSupport_contDiff`, `Analysis/Normed/Lp/SmoothApprox.lean`). Rademacher's theorem, `LipschitzWith.ae_differentiableAt`, and integration by parts against Lipschitz functions, `LipschitzWith.integral_lineDeriv_mul_eq`, are in `Analysis/Calculus/Rademacher.lean`.

### TauCeti

A weak directional derivative on an open set is defined by testing against Mathlib's bundled test functions. It is unique almost everywhere (`TauCeti.HasWeakFDerivOn.ae_eq`), agrees with a classical derivative when one exists, and survives \(L^1\) limits of the function and the derivative (`TauCeti.hasWeakFDerivOn_of_tendsto_lintegral_enorm_sub`) ([weak derivatives](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/WeakDeriv/Basic.lean), [limits](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/WeakDeriv/Limit.lean)).

Real-valued \(W^{1,p}(\Omega)\) is a closed space of \(L^p\) value-gradient pairs, identified with the weak-derivative predicate by `TauCeti.mem_w1pSubmodule_iff_hasWeakFDerivOn`, and \(W^{k,p}(\Omega)\) adds one weak derivative per order. Domains are open subsets of finite-dimensional real inner product spaces with an additive Haar measure. All these spaces are complete, and \(W^{1,2}\) is a Hilbert space. For \(k\geq2\) the chosen norm of \(W^{k,2}\) need not come from an inner product ([first order](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/W1p/Basic.lean), [higher order](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Wkp/Basic.lean)). Separability and reflexivity are not proved.

For \(1\leq p<\infty\), any open \(\Omega\) and any order \(k\), functions smooth on \(\Omega\) are dense in \(W^{k,p}(\Omega)\), with no boundedness or boundary regularity (Meyers–Serrin, `TauCeti.Wkp.dense_contDiffOn_representatives`) ([Meyers–Serrin](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Wkp/MeyersSerrin.lean)). Convolution with a smooth kernel turns a weak derivative into the classical derivative of the convolution wherever the translated kernel support lies in the domain ([mollification](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Mollification/Basic.lean), [interior form](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Mollification/Interior.lean)). Interior mollifications converge in \(W^{k,p}\) on compactly contained subdomains ([local approximation](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Wkp/Approximation.lean)). On the whole space, test functions are dense in \(W^{k,p}\) (`TauCeti.Wkp.denseRange_ofTestFunctionₗ_top`) ([density](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Wkp/Density.lean)). Smooth approximation in \(L^p\) by approximate identities is also proved ([approximate identities](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/Lp/ApproximateIdentity.lean)).

On an open interval and with values in a Banach space, \(u'\) is the weak derivative of \(u\) exactly when \(u\) is almost everywhere a constant plus the primitive of \(u'\) (`TauCeti.hasWeakLineDerivOn_Ioo_iff_exists_ae_eq_add_intervalIntegral`). For integrable \(u'\) there is a representative continuous on the closed interval that satisfies the fundamental theorem of calculus ([interval](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/WeakDeriv/Interval.lean)).

For \(1\leq p<\infty\), composition with a Lipschitz \(C^1\) function \(F\) with \(F(0)=0\) preserves \(W^{1,p}(\Omega)\), with \(\nabla(F\circ u)=F'(u)\nabla u\) (`TauCeti.W1p.hasWeakFDerivOn_comp`). The truncations \((u-k)^+\) are in \(W^{1,p}\) with gradient \(\mathbf 1_{\{u>k\}}\nabla u\) ([chain rule and truncation](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/W1p/ChainRule.lean)), and truncation is continuous in \(W^{1,p}\) and preserves \(W^{1,p}_0\) ([truncation](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/W1p/Truncation.lean)). The weak gradient vanishes almost everywhere on every level set (`TauCeti.W1p.gradient_ae_eq_zero_on_level_set`) ([level sets](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/W1p/LevelSet.lean)). The chain rule for Lipschitz \(F\) that is not \(C^1\) is not proved, apart from truncations.

The product rule holds for a smooth factor ([Leibniz](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Leibniz.lean)). Multiplication by a smooth function with bounded value and gradient is a bounded operator on \(W^{1,p}(\Omega)\) ([multiplication](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/W1p/Multiplication.lean), [higher order](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Wkp/Multiplication.lean)). The product of two Sobolev functions is treated only for \(p>n\) on the whole space (see the embeddings section). The algebra of bounded \(W^{1,p}\) functions is not proved.

Precomposition with a map \(\Phi\) that is differentiable on \(U\), with \(\|D\Phi\|\leq L\) and \(|\det D\Phi|\geq c>0\), is a bounded operator \(W^{1,p}(V)\to W^{1,p}(U)\) with the chain rule \(\nabla(u\circ\Phi)=D\Phi^*(\nabla u\circ\Phi)\) (`TauCeti.W1p.compL`) ([change of variables](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/W1p/ChangeOfVariables.lean)). The same holds for measure-preserving locally Lipschitz maps (`TauCeti.W1p.compMeasurePreservingL`) ([measure-preserving maps](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/W1p/MeasurePreserving.lean)). A general bi-Lipschitz map, differentiable only almost everywhere, is not treated.

Locally Lipschitz functions are weakly differentiable, with the almost-everywhere derivative of Rademacher's theorem as weak derivative (`LocallyLipschitzOn.hasWeakFDerivOn_fderiv`) ([Lipschitz functions](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/WeakDeriv/Lipschitz.lean)). Conversely, \(W^{1,\infty}(\Omega)\) functions have locally Lipschitz precise representatives (the case \(p=\infty\) of the interior Morrey inequality below). Difference quotients of \(W^{1,p}\) functions, \(1\leq p<\infty\), are bounded on compact subsets by the directional derivative ([difference quotients](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/W1p/DifferenceQuotient.lean)). The converse is proved only for \(p=2\): bounded \(L^2\) difference quotients give an \(L^2\) weak derivative ([converse](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/DifferenceQuotient.lean)).

For a bounded Lipschitz domain \(\Omega\subset\mathbb R\times E\) and \(1\leq p\leq\infty\), there is a bounded linear extension operator \(W^{1,p}(\Omega)\to W^{1,p}(\mathbb R\times E)\) with restriction as a left inverse (`TauCeti.W1p.exists_extensionL_of_isLipschitzDomainWith`). It is built from graph reflection in boundary charts and a smooth partition of unity ([extension](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/LipschitzDomain/GlobalExtension.lean), [chart extension](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/LipschitzDomain/Extension.lean)). Extension of order \(m\geq2\) is absent.

## Embeddings, norm inequalities, and compactness

[Mathematical scope](SYNOPSIS26.md#embeddings-norm-inequalities-and-compactness).

### Mathlib

**The Gagliardo–Nirenberg–Sobolev inequality.** Let \(E\) be a finite-dimensional real normed space of dimension \(n\) with a Haar measure, and let \(u\) be of class \(C^1\) (`ContDiff ℝ 1`). The derivative is the Fréchet derivative in operator norm (`Analysis/FunctionalSpaces/SobolevInequality.lean`).

For the coordinate sup norm on \(\iota\to\mathbb R\) with \(n=\#\iota\geq2\), compact support, \(p=n/(n-1)\), and a real normed codomain,
\[
\int \|u\|^p \leq \Bigl(\int \|Du\|\Bigr)^p.
\]
On a general \(E\) of dimension \(n\geq2\) the same bound holds with a constant depending only on \(E\), the Haar measure and \(p\). This constant comes from comparing the Haar measure with Lebesgue measure and from a chosen linear equivalence \(E\simeq\mathbb R^n\); it is not evaluated further.

For \(1\leq p<n\) and \(1/p'=1/p-1/n\), a compactly supported \(u\) satisfies \(\|u\|_{L^{p'}}\leq C\|Du\|_{L^p}\) (`MeasureTheory.eLpNorm_le_eLpNorm_fderiv_of_eq`, and `MeasureTheory.eLpNorm_le_eLpNorm_fderiv_of_eq_inner` for an inner-product codomain). For a codomain that is a real inner product space of any dimension, \(C\) depends only on \(E\), the measure and \(p\). Completeness of the codomain is not a hypothesis. For a finite-dimensional normed codomain, \(C\) may also depend on the codomain. The case \(p=1\) holds for an arbitrary real normed codomain (`MeasureTheory.eLpNorm_le_eLpNorm_fderiv_one`). If instead the support lies in a bounded set \(s\) and \(p^{-1}-n^{-1}\leq q^{-1}\), then \(\|u\|_{L^q}\leq C\|Du\|_{L^p}\) with \(C\) depending on \(s\), \(p\), \(q\), the codomain and the measure (`MeasureTheory.eLpNorm_le_eLpNorm_fderiv_of_le`). All of these are inequalities for \(C^1\) functions, not embeddings of a Sobolev space.

### TauCeti

For \(1\leq p<n\) and the Sobolev exponent \(p^*\), \(\|u\|_{p^*}\leq C\|\nabla u\|_p\) on \(W^{1,p}_0(\Omega)\) for every open \(\Omega\). The bound is packaged as a continuous linear map \(W^{1,p}_0(\Omega)\to L^{p^*}(\Omega)\) ([Sobolev embedding](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Embedding.lean)). Since \(W^{1,p}_0(\mathbb R^n)=W^{1,p}(\mathbb R^n)\) for \(p<\infty\) (`TauCeti.w1p0Submodule_top_eq_top`) ([density](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/W1p/Density.lean)), this includes the whole-space embedding. The inhomogeneous estimate on Lipschitz domains is not stated. On sets of finite measure, \(W^{1,p}_0(\Omega)\hookrightarrow L^q\) holds whenever \(1/p-1/q<1/n\), with an explicit constant, in every dimension ([finite measure](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/FiniteMeasure.lean)). The \(L^p\)–\(L^q\) bound for Riesz potentials on sets of finite measure (Gilbarg–Trudinger, Lemma 7.12) underlies this ([Riesz potentials](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Integral/RieszPotential.lean)).

In the borderline case \(p=n\), \(W^{1,n}_0(\Omega)\) embeds into \(L^q(\Omega)\) for every finite \(q\geq n\) on sets of finite measure, with a constant of order \(q^{1-1/n}\). Trudinger's inequality \(\int_\Omega\exp\bigl((|u|/(c\|Du\|_{L^n}))^{n/(n-1)}\bigr)\leq e(n^n+5)\mu(\Omega)\) also holds (`TauCeti.W1p.setLIntegral_exp_abs_value_div_rpow_le`) ([Trudinger](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Trudinger.lean)). Without boundary conditions, \(W^{1,n}(\Omega)\) has bounded mean oscillation on balls in \(\Omega\), with local exponential integrability ([BMO](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/W1p/BoundedMeanOscillation.lean)). The failure of \(W^{1,n}\subset L^\infty\) for \(n\geq2\) is not proved.

For \(n<p<\infty\), every \(u\in W^{1,p}(\mathbb R^n)\) has a Hölder representative of exponent \(1-n/p\) with constant \(C\|\nabla u\|_p\) (`TauCeti.W1p.exists_holderWith_ae_eq_value`) ([Morrey](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/W1p/Morrey.lean)). This representative gives a bounded map into the global Hölder space (`TauCeti.W1p.morreyEmbedding`) ([Hölder embedding](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/W1p/HolderEmbedding.lean)). For \(n<p\leq\infty\) and any open \(\Omega\), the precise representative of \(u\in W^{1,p}(\Omega)\) is locally Hölder of exponent \(1-n/p\) (`TauCeti.W1p.exists_holderOnWith_preciseRepresentative`) ([interior Morrey](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/W1p/InteriorMorrey.lean)). The embedding into \(C^{0,1-n/p}(\overline\Omega)\) on bounded Lipschitz domains is not stated. Morrey's inequality for \(C^1\) functions is also proved, with an explicit constant ([Morrey for \(C^1\)](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Morrey.lean)).

Rellich–Kondrachov compactness of \(W^{1,p}_0(\Omega)\to L^p(\Omega)\) holds on bounded open sets, \(1\leq p<\infty\) (`TauCeti.W1p0.isCompactOperator_valueL`) ([compactness](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/RellichKondrachov.lean)). Restriction to a compactly contained subdomain is compact without boundary conditions ([local compactness](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/W1p/LocalCompactness.lean)), also for finite-dimensional vector-valued fields ([vector-valued](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/GraphStep/LocalCompactness.lean)). On bounded Lipschitz domains, \(W^{1,p}(\Omega)\to L^p(\Omega)\) is compact (`TauCeti.W1p.isCompactOperator_valueL_of_isLipschitzDomainWith`) ([Lipschitz domains](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/LipschitzDomain/RellichKondrachov.lean)). Compactness into \(L^q\) for \(p<q<p^*\), and into Hölder spaces for \(p>n\), is not proved.

Poincaré inequalities are proved in the following forms:
- On \(W^{1,p}_0(\Omega)\), \(1\leq p\leq\infty\), for \(\Omega\) contained in a slab of width \(b-a\), with constant \(b-a\) (`TauCeti.W1p.norm_value_le_mul_norm_gradient_of_subset_slab`) ([slab](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Poincare/W1p0.lean), [\(C^1\) form](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Poincare/Slab.lean)).
- On sets of finite measure, the case \(q=p\) of the finite-measure embedding above.
- The Poincaré–Wirtinger inequality on bounded convex domains, with constant \(\mu(B(0,1))(\mathop{\mathrm{diam}}\Omega)^{n+1}/\mu(S)\) (`TauCeti.W1p.eLpNorm_value_sub_setAverage_le_of_convex`) ([convex domains](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Poincare/Wirtinger/W1p.lean)).
- The Poincaré–Wirtinger inequality on bounded connected Lipschitz domains, with a nonexplicit constant (`TauCeti.W1p.exists_eLpNorm_value_sub_setAverage_le_of_isLipschitzDomainWith`). It follows from compactness on preconnected domains ([from compactness](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Poincare/Wirtinger/Compactness.lean)).
- The failure of every such inequality on the whole space (`TauCeti.not_exists_eLpNorm_le_const_mul_eLpNorm_fderiv`) ([whole space](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Poincare/WholeSpace.lean)).
- De Giorgi's isoperimetric inequality on convex domains ([De Giorgi](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Poincare/Wirtinger/DeGiorgi.lean)).

For \(p>n\), \(W^{1,p}(\mathbb R^n)\) is closed under products, with the Leibniz rule and \(\|uv\|\leq C\|u\|\|v\|\) (`TauCeti.W1p.mulL`, `TauCeti.W1p.norm_mul_le`) ([supercritical multiplication](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/W1p/SupercriticalMultiplication.lean)). The higher-order algebra theorem is absent.

The sufficiency direction of the Fréchet–Kolmogorov criterion is proved for \(1\leq p<\infty\) on proper normed groups with Haar measure and finite-dimensional values: bounded families with uniformly small translations that are uniformly tight are totally bounded ([Fréchet–Kolmogorov](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/Lp/FrechetKolmogorov.lean)). The necessity direction is not proved.

The interpolation inequality \(\|\nabla u\|_p\leq\varepsilon\|D^2u\|_p+(n^2/\varepsilon)\|u\|_p\) holds on \(W^{2,p}(E)\) and on \(W^{2,p}_0(\Omega)\), \(1\leq p<\infty\) (`TauCeti.Wkp.norm_iteratedGradient_lowerOrder_le`) ([interpolation inequality](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Wkp/Interpolation.lean)). This is an explicit instance of the Ehrling inequality; the abstract Ehrling lemma is not proved. For Aubin–Lions, the relevant supporting material is the energy space \(L^2(a,b;V)\cap H^1(a,b;V^*)\) of a Gelfand triple, with continuous representatives in the pivot space ([evolution space](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/EvolutionSpace/Basic.lean), [Gelfand triple](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/WeakDeriv/GelfandTriple.lean)). No compactness theorem of Aubin–Lions type is proved.

The whole-space embedding \(W^{k,2}(\mathbb R^n)\to C_0(\mathbb R^n)\) for \(2k>n\) is recorded under the Besov heading below.

Its value and highest weak derivative are the zero extensions (`TauCeti.Wkp0.extendByZeroₗᵢ`) ([order \(k\)](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Wkp/Extension.lean), [order one](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/W1p/Extension.lean)). ## Besov and Triebel–Lizorkin scales

[Mathematical scope](SYNOPSIS26.md#besov-and-triebellizorkin-scales).

### Mathlib

**Bessel potential spaces.** On a finite-dimensional real inner product space \(E\) with Lebesgue measure, and for a complete complex normed space \(F\), the Bessel potential of order \(s\in\mathbb R\) is the Fourier multiplier on tempered distributions with symbol \((1+\|x\|^2)^{s/2}\) (`TemperedDistribution.besselPotential`). Because of the normalization of the Fourier transform, this is \((1-(2\pi)^{-2}\Delta)^{s/2}\), not \((1-\Delta)^{s/2}\). Potentials compose additively in the order (`Analysis/Distribution/Sobolev.lean`). For \(1\leq p\leq\infty\), a tempered distribution lies in the Sobolev class of order \(s\) and exponent \(p\) when its potential is represented by an \(L^p\) function (`TemperedDistribution.MemSobolev`). Every Schwartz function lies in every class, and the classes are complex vector spaces.

The bundled space \(H^{s,p}(E,F)\) (`BesselPotentialSpace`, `Analysis/FunctionalSpaces/BesselPotentialSpace.lean`) consists of a tempered distribution together with the \(L^p\) function produced by its potential. The map to \(L^p\) is a linear isometric equivalence (`BesselPotentialSpace.toLpₗᵢ`), so \(H^{s,p}\) is a Banach space. For \(p=2\) and an inner-product codomain, \(H^s\) is a Hilbert space. This isometry is the lifting property of the Bessel scale. For \(p=2\) there is a Fourier-side characterization. In that case the scale is monotone in \(s\), Fourier multiplication by a bounded function of temperate growth preserves \(H^{s,2}\), directional derivatives map \(H^{s,2}\) to \(H^{s-1,2}\), and the Laplacian maps it to \(H^{s-2,2}\). If \(s>\dim E/2\) and \(u\in H^{s,2}\), then \(\widehat u\) is integrable (`TemperedDistribution.MemSobolev.fourier_memL1`).

**Pointwise Hölder regularity.** For \(k\in\mathbb N\) and \(\alpha\in[0,1]\), a map is of class \(C^{k+(\alpha)}\) at \(a\) when it is \(C^k\) at \(a\) and \(D^kf(x)-D^kf(a)=O(\|x-a\|^\alpha)\) as \(x\to a\) (`ContDiffPointwiseHolderAt`, `Analysis/Calculus/ContDiffHolder/Pointwise.lean`). This is the pointwise condition at one point, not Hölder continuity on a neighborhood. The class \(C^{k+(0)}\) is \(C^k\). \(C^n\) at \(a\) with \(n>k\) implies \(C^{k+(\alpha)}\), and lowering \(k\) or \(\alpha\) preserves the class. A neighborhood Hölder bound on \(D^kf\) implies the pointwise class. The class is stable under composition (for \(k=0\), when one map is differentiable at the relevant point), under continuous linear maps and products, and under taking derivatives of lower order. The two-point Hölder condition on a set, with a Hölder seminorm, is in `Topology/MetricSpace/Holder.lean` and `Topology/MetricSpace/HolderNorm.lean`.

### TauCeti

On the whole space, for every natural \(k\) and \(1<p<\infty\), a real \(L^p\) function lies in Mathlib's \(H^{k,p}(\mathbb R^n)\) exactly when it is the value of an element of \(W^{k,p}(\mathbb R^n)\) (Calderón's theorem, `MeasureTheory.Lp.memSobolev_natCast_iff_exists_wkp_value_eq`). The proof goes through the Mihlin–Hörmander theorem of Section 27 ([Bessel and Sobolev spaces](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/BesselPotential/Basic.lean)). For \(s>n/2\), every element of \(H^{s,2}\) has a representative in \(C_0(\mathbb R^n)\), and for \(2k>n\) the map \(W^{k,2}(\mathbb R^n)\to C_0(\mathbb R^n)\) is bounded ([embedding into \(C_0\)](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/BesselPotential/Embedding.lean)).

There are complete spaces of bounded global \(C^{0,\alpha}\), \(C^{1,\alpha}\) and \(C^{2,\alpha}\) functions (`TauCeti.HolderSpace` and its higher-order analogues), and the order-zero space is a Banach algebra for Banach-algebra-valued functions ([Hölder spaces](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Holder/Normed.lean), [algebra](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Holder/Algebra.lean), [first order](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Holder/One.lean), [second order](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Holder/Two.lean)). Smooth dyadic Littlewood–Paley cutoffs \(\psi_j(\xi)=\varphi(2^{-j}\xi)-\varphi(2^{1-j}\xi)\) form a partition of unity of \(E\setminus\{0\}\) with \(\|D^k\psi_j\|\leq B2^{-jk}\) ([Littlewood–Paley cutoffs](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/BumpFunction/LittlewoodPaley.lean)). Besov and Triebel–Lizorkin spaces themselves are not defined.

**Synopsis entries.** Partly covered: 26.46 (\(W^{m,p}=H^{m,p}\) for integer \(m\) and \(1<p<\infty\)). Not covered: 26.43, 26.45, 26.47–26.52, 26.78.

## Real and complex interpolation

[Mathematical scope](SYNOPSIS26.md#real-and-complex-interpolation).

### Mathlib

Hadamard's three-lines theorem for bounded functions on a closed vertical strip, holomorphic inside, is proved with values in a complex normed space (`Analysis/Complex/Hadamard.lean`, namespace `Complex.HadamardThreeLines`). It is the analytic ingredient of complex interpolation. Banach couples and the real and complex interpolation functors are not defined.

### TauCeti

The Riesz–Thorin theorem is proved for complex-linear operators from simple functions to measurable functions. Bounds \(L^{p_0}\to L^{q_0}\) with norm \(M_0\) and \(L^{p_1}\to L^{q_1}\) with norm \(M_1\) give \(\|Tf\|_q\leq M_0^{1-\theta}M_1^\theta\|f\|_p\) at the intermediate exponents, for simple \(f\) (`TauCeti.eLpNorm_le_rpow_mul_rpow_mul_eLpNorm`). Source exponents lie in \((0,\infty]\) and target exponents in \([1,\infty]\). The target measure is σ-finite, a hypothesis needed only when \(q=\infty\) ([Riesz–Thorin](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Integral/RieszThorin.lean)). The duality lemma it uses, norming \(L^q\) by simple test functions, is separate ([duality](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/Lp/Duality.lean)).

Marcinkiewicz interpolation is proved for sublinear operators in two diagonal cases. The first has weak type \((p_0,p_0)\) and \((p_1,p_1)\) with \(0<p_0<p_1<\infty\), and gives strong type \((p,p)\) for \(p_0<p<p_1\) ([finite endpoints](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Integral/Marcinkiewicz/General.lean)). The second has weak type \((1,1)\) together with boundedness on \(L^\infty\), and gives strong type \((p,p)\) for \(1<p<\infty\) ([weak \((1,1)\) and \(L^\infty\)](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Integral/Marcinkiewicz/Basic.lean)). The case of a weak-type \((p_0,p_0)\) endpoint with \(p_0>1\) and an \(L^\infty\) endpoint is not stated, and neither is the off-diagonal theorem. Weak \(L^p\) and Lorentz spaces are not defined.

**Synopsis entries.** Covered: 26.58 (TauCeti, for operators defined on simple functions). Partly covered: 26.59 (finite weak-type endpoints, and weak type \((1,1)\) with \(L^\infty\)). Not covered: 26.53–26.57, 26.60–26.65, 26.79, 26.80.

## Morrey and Campanato descriptions

[Mathematical scope](SYNOPSIS26.md#morrey-and-campanato-descriptions).

### TauCeti

Campanato's theorem is proved with the \(L^1\) mean oscillation on an additive Haar space. For \(0<\alpha\), a locally integrable function agrees almost everywhere with an \(\alpha\)-Hölder function exactly when its mean oscillation over balls of radius \(r\) is \(O(r^\alpha)\). The Hölder representative is the precise representative (`TauCeti.MeasureTheory.exists_holderWith_ae_eq_iff_forall_setAverage_norm_sub_le`) ([Campanato](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/Campanato.lean)). The \(L^p\) Campanato seminorms for \(p>1\) are not treated.

The John–Nirenberg inequality is proved on dyadic cubes and on sup-norm balls in \(\mathbb R^n\). It gives the distribution bound \(|\{|f-f_{Q_0}|>s\}|\leq2e^{-cs/M}|Q_0|\) and local exponential integrability (`TauCeti.volume_lt_norm_sub_setAverage_le`, `TauCeti.setLIntegral_exp_mul_norm_sub_setAverage_le`) ([John–Nirenberg](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Integral/JohnNirenberg.lean)). The equivalence of the \(L^p\) Campanato seminorms with the BMO seminorm is not stated.

Morrey's Dirichlet growth theorem is proved for \(u\in W^{1,p}(\Omega)\), \(p\geq1\), and \(\alpha>0\). If \(\int_{B(x,r)}\|\nabla u\|\leq Mr^{n-1+\alpha}\) on the relevant balls, then the precise representative is \(\alpha\)-Hölder with constant a multiple of \(M\) (`TauCeti.W1p.dist_preciseRepresentative_le_of_setIntegral_norm_gradient_le`) ([Dirichlet growth](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/W1p/DirichletGrowth.lean)).

Morrey spaces are not defined, and Adams' theorem is not proved. The Riesz potential bounds on sets of finite measure in the embeddings section are supporting material for 26.83.

**Synopsis entries.** Covered: 26.82 (TauCeti). Partly covered: 26.73 (the \(L^1\) Campanato seminorm), 26.74 (the John–Nirenberg inequality). Not covered: 26.83.

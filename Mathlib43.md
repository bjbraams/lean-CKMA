# 43. Univalent Functions, Conformal Mapping, and Extremal Methods

Checked against Mathlib commit `f039e1b8a616d45782a5c7fc5e7909249c36b8ea` (2026-10-18, Lean `v4.36.0-rc1`). The checkout was only read. The survey below concerns this Mathlib revision; the TauCeti supplement and final gap list also use the [TauCeti source audit](TauCetiCoverage.md).

**Imports.** There is no aggregate module. A file `Mathlib/A/B/C.lean` is imported as `import Mathlib.A.B.C`. The roots for this section are `Mathlib.Analysis.Normed.Operator.Conformal`, `Mathlib.Analysis.Calculus.Conformal.NormedSpace`, `Mathlib.Analysis.Complex.Conformal`, `Mathlib.Analysis.InnerProductSpace.ConformalLinearMap`, `Mathlib.Geometry.Euclidean.Angle.Unoriented.Conformal`, and `Mathlib.Geometry.Manifold.ConformalGroupoid`.

**Namespaces.** Conformality of a continuous linear map is `IsConformalMap`. Conformality of a differentiable map at a point is `ConformalAt`, and conformality at every point is `Conformal`. The conformal maps of a normed space form `conformalGroupoid`.

What is proved is the infinitesimal meaning of a conformal map, not the theory of univalent functions. The Riemann mapping theorem is not claimed; Section 40 records that the file of that name holds only lemmas strictly weaker than the theorem.

A continuous linear map between normed spaces over a normed field is conformal when it is a nonzero scalar multiple of a linear isometry (`Analysis/Normed/Operator/Conformal.lean`). A map between real normed spaces is conformal at a point when it is real-differentiable there and its differential is a conformal linear map. Compositions of conformal maps are conformal, and so is multiplication by a nonzero constant (`Analysis/Calculus/Conformal/NormedSpace.lean`). Between real inner-product spaces, a linear map is conformal if and only if it multiplies the inner product by a positive constant, and the differential of a map conformal at a point preserves angles (`Analysis/InnerProductSpace/ConformalLinearMap.lean`, `Geometry/Euclidean/Angle/Unoriented/Conformal.lean`).

For the complex plane the criterion is Cauchy–Riemann. A nonzero complex-linear map from \(\mathbb{C}\) into a complex normed space is conformal, and so is its composition with complex conjugation. A real-linear map from \(\mathbb{C}\) to itself is conformal if and only if it is nonzero and is either complex-linear or complex-linear after conjugation. A real-differentiable map from the plane into a complex normed space, with nonvanishing differential, is conformal at a point if it is holomorphic there. Between open sets of the plane, a real-differentiable map with nonvanishing differential is conformal at a point if and only if it is holomorphic or antiholomorphic there. Antiholomorphic maps, including complex conjugation, are counted as conformal. The file notes that it is not yet proved that a map conformal at every point of a connected open set is holomorphic throughout or antiholomorphic throughout (`Analysis/Complex/Conformal.lean`).

The conformal local homeomorphisms of a normed space are the morphisms of a structure groupoid, used as the atlas of a conformal manifold (`Geometry/Manifold/ConformalGroupoid.lean`).

Carathéodory's continuity theorem is proved: a conformal map of a disc onto a bounded region extends continuously to the closed disc if and only if the boundary of the region is locally connected, in particular when it is a Jordan curve, and the extension maps the closed disc onto the closure of the region (`exists_continuousOn_closedBall_eqOn_iff_locallyConnectedSpace_frontier`, `exists_continuousOn_closedBall_bijOn_ball_of_isJordanCurve_frontier`). Conversely, a bounded domain carried by a conformal map onto a disc, with continuous injective extension to the closure, is a Jordan domain ([Jordan domains](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/Jordan/Domain.lean)).

Schwarz–Christoffel primitives and polygon mapping theorems are proved. **Schwarz–Christoffel theorem:** if \(U\) is a bounded domain whose frontier is a Jordan curve and which is polygonal, coinciding near each vertex \(v_i\) with a sector of opening \((e_i+1)\pi\), \(e_i\in(-1,1)\), and with a half-plane near every other boundary point, then there are distinct real prevertices \(a_i\) and constants \(A\neq0\), \(B\) such that \(AF+B\) maps the upper half-plane bijectively onto \(U\) and sends \(a_i\) to \(v_i\), where \(F\) is the primitive of \(\prod_i(z-a_i)^{e_i}\); the exponents sum to \(-2\). Analogous statements hold for unbounded polygonal domains with a sector, half-strip or half-strip-exterior end (`exists_bijOn_const_mul_schwarzChristoffelPrimitive_add_of_isJordanCurve_frontier`, [polygonal Jordan domains](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/SchwarzChristoffel/JordanPolygon.lean)), and the prevertices are unique up to an automorphism of the half-plane ([prevertex uniqueness](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/SchwarzChristoffel/PrevertexUniqueness.lean)). The existence statement does not compute the prevertices from the polygon. The constructive direction is also present: for given prevertices and exponents with \(\sum_i|e_i|\le1\) the primitive is injective ([univalence criterion](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/SchwarzChristoffel/Univalence.lean)), and finite signed-height checks on the vertices certify a simple nonconvex polygon, possibly with reentrant corners, onto whose interior the primitive maps bijectively ([nonconvex mapping](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/SchwarzChristoffel/Polygon/Nonconvex/Mapping.lean)).

Two names require care. `Koebe.lean` proves the square-root extremal step for Riemann mapping, not Koebe's one-quarter theorem. `Area.lean` proves the injective holomorphic change-of-variables identity, not the coefficient area theorem of univalent-function theory ([Koebe step](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/Koebe.lean), [area identity](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/Area.lean)).

The length–area method, the classical ingredient of extremal-length arguments, is proved: for a holomorphic map with finite Dirichlet integral on an annulus, some circle in the annulus has an image of controlled length, together with Wolff's lemma, for an arbitrary measurable weight ([holomorphic length–area](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/LengthArea.lean), [measure-theoretic form](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Integral/CircleLIntegral.lean)). Extremal length as a conformal invariant of curve families is not defined.

A function on a convex set whose derivative, after a fixed rotation, has positive real part is injective (Noshiro–Warschawski, [univalence](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Univalence.lean)). Two holomorphic functions with nonvanishing derivatives and equal pre-Schwarzian derivative \(f''/f'\) on a domain differ by an affine postcomposition ([pre-Schwarzian](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/PreSchwarzian.lean)). Crosscuts of a domain and their separation properties are defined, as a first step toward prime ends ([crosscuts](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/PrimeEnd/Crosscut/Basic.lean)); prime ends themselves and the prime-end compactification are not constructed.

The upgrade to a homeomorphism of closures is proved when the continuous disc extension has connected boundary fibres: this hypothesis forces boundary injectivity. The connected-fibre property is not derived for every Jordan-domain Riemann map, so the homeomorphism of closures for Jordan domains remains conditional ([conditional injectivity](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/MonotoneExtension.lean)).

## Topics of Section 43 not found in either inspected library

- The Koebe one-quarter theorem, the growth and distortion theorems, the coefficient area theorem for \(\Sigma\), Grunsky inequalities, and the Bieberbach/de Branges theorem. Holomorphic injections and substantial conformal mapping theory are present in TauCeti.
- Extremal length, quadratic differentials, and the coefficient body. The length–area inequality is present in TauCeti.
- Löwner chains and the Löwner–Kufarev differential equation, subordination, and harmonic measure.
- Nehari's Schwarzian-derivative univalence criterion; TauCeti has the pre-Schwarzian composition rule and rigidity and the Noshiro–Warschawski criterion.
- Prime ends and the prime-end correspondence; TauCeti defines crosscuts only.
- The unconditional Carathéodory–Osgood theorem that the Riemann map of a Jordan domain extends to a homeomorphism of the closures; TauCeti proves the continuous extension onto the closure and the homeomorphism under the connected-boundary-fibre hypothesis.
- The statement that a map conformal at every point of a connected open set in the plane is entirely holomorphic or entirely antiholomorphic. The file marks it as not yet proved.

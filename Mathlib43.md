# 43. Univalent Functions, Conformal Mapping, and Extremal Methods

Checked against Mathlib commit `065356127b1dc0016f66b7283ce0ce2c4055aa55` (2026-09-16, Lean `v4.35.0-rc2`). The checkout was only read. The survey below concerns this Mathlib revision; the TauCeti supplement and final gap list also use the [TauCeti source audit](TauCetiCoverage.md).

**Imports.** There is no aggregate module. A file `Mathlib/A/B/C.lean` is imported as `import Mathlib.A.B.C`. The roots for this section are `Mathlib.Analysis.Normed.Operator.Conformal`, `Mathlib.Analysis.Calculus.Conformal.NormedSpace`, `Mathlib.Analysis.Complex.Conformal`, `Mathlib.Analysis.InnerProductSpace.ConformalLinearMap`, `Mathlib.Geometry.Euclidean.Angle.Unoriented.Conformal`, and `Mathlib.Geometry.Manifold.ConformalGroupoid`.

**Namespaces.** Conformality of a continuous linear map is `IsConformalMap`. Conformality of a differentiable map at a point is `ConformalAt`, and conformality at every point is `Conformal`. The conformal maps of a normed space form `conformalGroupoid`.

What is proved is the infinitesimal meaning of a conformal map, not the theory of univalent functions. The Riemann mapping theorem is not claimed; Section 40 records that the file of that name holds only lemmas strictly weaker than the theorem.

A continuous linear map between normed spaces over a normed field is conformal when it is a nonzero scalar multiple of a linear isometry (`Analysis/Normed/Operator/Conformal.lean`). A map between real normed spaces is conformal at a point when it is real-differentiable there and its differential is a conformal linear map. Compositions of conformal maps are conformal, and so is multiplication by a nonzero constant (`Analysis/Calculus/Conformal/NormedSpace.lean`). Between real inner-product spaces, a linear map is conformal if and only if it multiplies the inner product by a positive constant, and the differential of a map conformal at a point preserves angles (`Analysis/InnerProductSpace/ConformalLinearMap.lean`, `Geometry/Euclidean/Angle/Unoriented/Conformal.lean`).

For the complex plane the criterion is Cauchy–Riemann. A nonzero complex-linear map from \(\mathbb{C}\) into a complex normed space is conformal, and so is its composition with complex conjugation. A real-linear map from \(\mathbb{C}\) to itself is conformal if and only if it is nonzero and is either complex-linear or complex-linear after conjugation. A real-differentiable map from the plane into a complex normed space, with nonvanishing differential, is conformal at a point if it is holomorphic there. Between open sets of the plane, a real-differentiable map with nonvanishing differential is conformal at a point if and only if it is holomorphic or antiholomorphic there. Antiholomorphic maps, including complex conjugation, are counted as conformal. The file notes that it is not yet proved that a map conformal at every point of a connected open set is holomorphic throughout or antiholomorphic throughout (`Analysis/Complex/Conformal.lean`).

The conformal local homeomorphisms of a normed space are the morphisms of a structure groupoid, used as the atlas of a conformal manifold (`Geometry/Manifold/ConformalGroupoid.lean`).

Disc automorphisms and Schwarz–Pick contraction/rigidity are developed ([Schwarz–Pick](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6e53de0d3ce9ea24d7d487e656ac4590b0c45b3a/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/SchwarzPick/Basic.lean), [rigidity](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6e53de0d3ce9ea24d7d487e656ac4590b0c45b3a/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/SchwarzPick/Rigidity.lean)). For bounded Jordan domains, a conformal map extends continuously to the closed disc, and the continuous extension maps the boundary onto the image boundary ([Carathéodory](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6e53de0d3ce9ea24d7d487e656ac4590b0c45b3a/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/Caratheodory.lean), [boundary correspondence](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6e53de0d3ce9ea24d7d487e656ac4590b0c45b3a/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/BoundaryCorrespondence.lean)).

Schwarz–Christoffel primitives and polygon mapping theorems are proved. In particular, the nonconvex polygon theorem allows reentrant corners under explicit exponent conditions and finite signed-height checks ensuring a simple boundary; it is not an unconditional solution of the parameter problem for arbitrary prescribed polygons ([nonconvex mapping](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6e53de0d3ce9ea24d7d487e656ac4590b0c45b3a/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/SchwarzChristoffel/Polygon/Nonconvex/Mapping.lean)).

Two names require care. `Koebe.lean` proves the square-root extremal step for Riemann mapping, not Koebe's one-quarter theorem. `Area.lean` proves the injective holomorphic change-of-variables identity, not the coefficient area theorem of univalent-function theory ([Koebe step](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6e53de0d3ce9ea24d7d487e656ac4590b0c45b3a/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/Koebe.lean), [area identity](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6e53de0d3ce9ea24d7d487e656ac4590b0c45b3a/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/Area.lean)).

The length–area method, the classical ingredient of extremal-length arguments, is proved: for a holomorphic map with finite Dirichlet integral on an annulus, some circle in the annulus has an image of controlled length, together with Wolff's lemma, for an arbitrary measurable weight ([holomorphic length–area](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6e53de0d3ce9ea24d7d487e656ac4590b0c45b3a/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/LengthArea.lean), [measure-theoretic form](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6e53de0d3ce9ea24d7d487e656ac4590b0c45b3a/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Integral/CircleLIntegral.lean)). Extremal length as a conformal invariant of curve families is not defined.

The upgrade to a homeomorphism of closures is proved when the continuous disc extension has connected boundary fibres: this hypothesis forces boundary injectivity. It is not simply assumed here that the connected-fibre property has been derived for every Jordan-domain Riemann map ([conditional injectivity](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6e53de0d3ce9ea24d7d487e656ac4590b0c45b3a/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/MonotoneExtension.lean)).

## Topics of Section 43 not found in either inspected library

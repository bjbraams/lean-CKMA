For transport, `TauCeti.IsContinuityEquation` defines distributional solutions of $\partial_t\mu_t+\operatorname{div}(v_t\mu_t)=0$ for a measurable family of measures on a finite-dimensional space with integrable mass and integrable $\int\|v_t\|\,d\mu_t$ ([source](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/ContinuityEquation/Basic.lean)). This is the time derivative of a quadratic Hopf–Lax envelope, without a viscosity-solution theory of the Hamilton–Jacobi equation. The Boltzmann entropy on Wasserstein space ([source](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Wasserstein/BoltzmannEntropy.lean)) is a functional of densities; no collision operator is defined.

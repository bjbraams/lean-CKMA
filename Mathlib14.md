## Rearrangement

The rearrangement inequality of Section 11 compares \(\sum_i f_i g_{\sigma i}\) with \(\sum_i f_i g_i\): the sum is maximized when \(f\) and \(g\) monovary and minimized when they antivary, for a permutation that moves only points of the set of summation (`Algebra/Order/Rearrangement.lean`).

## The Gagliardo–Nirenberg–Sobolev inequality

The full development belongs to Section 26. The statements proved in `Analysis/FunctionalSpaces/SobolevInequality.lean` are as follows. On \(\iota\to\mathbb{R}\) with \(\iota\) finite of cardinality \(n\ge 2\), if \(p\) is the Hölder conjugate of \(n\) and \(u\) is a \(C^1\) compactly supported map into a real normed space, then
\[
\int^- \|u\|^p\le\Bigl(\int^- \|Du\|\Bigr)^p,
\]
with constant \(1\), using the coordinate sup norm on \(\iota\to\mathbb R\) and its induced operator norm on \(Du\). The integrals are lower integrals of extended norms. On a finite-dimensional real normed space of dimension \(n\ge 2\), with a Haar measure, the same comparison holds with a constant that depends only on the space, the measure, and \(p\); the constant comes from the comparison of Haar measure with Lebesgue measure on \(\mathbb{R}^n\) and from the operator norm of a chosen linear equivalence with \(\mathbb{R}^n\). Equivalently, the \(L^p\) norm of \(u\) is at most a constant times the \(L^1\) norm of its Fréchet derivative.

If the codomain is a real inner product space, \(1\le p<n\), and \(p'=np/(n-p)>0\) is the Sobolev conjugate defined by \((p')^{-1}=p^{-1}-n^{-1}\), then the \(L^{p'}\) norm of a \(C^1\) compactly supported \(u\) is at most a constant, depending on the measure and on \(p\), times the \(L^p\) norm of the derivative. If instead the codomain is finite-dimensional, the support of \(u\) lies in a bounded set \(s\), and \(1\le p<n\), then the \(L^p\) norm of \(u\) is at most a constant, depending on the codomain, the measure, \(s\), and \(p\), times the \(L^p\) norm of the derivative. This last statement is a Poincaré inequality for functions supported in a fixed bounded set.

## Additional coverage in TauCeti

The following additions were checked in TauCeti revision `6ebb29f84b4d`; see the [revision, build evidence, and comparison scope](TauCetiCoverage.md). Source links below are pinned to that revision.

TauCeti extends the smooth-function Sobolev estimates to actual weak Sobolev spaces. For \(1\le p<n\), it proves \(W^{1,p}_0(\Omega)\hookrightarrow L^{np/(n-p)}(\Omega)\), with a gradient-norm bound ([Sobolev embedding](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Embedding.lean)). For \(n<p<\infty\), it constructs a continuous linear embedding of whole-space \(W^{1,p}(\mathbb R^n)\) into the global Hölder space of exponent \(1-n/p\) ([Morrey embedding](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/W1p/HolderEmbedding.lean)). The zero-boundary condition in the first result and the whole-space domain in the second are part of the statements. Section 26 records the underlying spaces and compactness results.

A Poincaré inequality is proved on \(W^{1,p}_0(\Omega)\), \(1\le p\le\infty\), when \(\Omega\) lies in a slab of width \(b-a\): \(\|u\|_p\le(b-a)\|\nabla u\|_p\). The constant does not depend on the dimension, on \(p\), or on the shape of \(\Omega\); it rests on the same estimate for \(C^1\) functions supported in the slab ([slab](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Poincare/Slab.lean)). A ball-containment version follows ([Poincaré](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Poincare/W1p0.lean)). The inequality fails for \(W^{1,p}_0\) of the whole space, and this is proved as well ([whole space](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Poincare/WholeSpace.lean)).

The Poincaré–Wirtinger inequality is proved on a bounded convex open set \(\Omega\) in a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, for \(1\le p<\infty\): for \(u\in W^{1,p}(\Omega)\) and a subset \(S\subseteq\Omega\) of positive measure,
\[
\Bigl\|u-\fint_S u\Bigr\|_{L^p(\Omega)}\le \frac{|B_1|\,(\operatorname{diam}\Omega)^{n+1}}{|S|}\,\|\nabla u\|_{L^p(\Omega)},
\]
with the constant \(2^{n+1}R\) on a ball of radius \(R\) averaged over the whole ball. The \(C^1\) version comes first, by the classical potential estimate ([C¹ version](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Poincare/Wirtinger/Basic.lean), [Sobolev version](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Poincare/Wirtinger/W1p.lean)). The constants are explicit, not sharp.

On the same bounded convex domains, De Giorgi's isoperimetric inequality for \(u\in W^{1,p}(\Omega)\), \(1\le p\le\infty\), and levels \(k<l\) reads \((l-k)\,|\{u\ge l\}|\,|\{u\le k\}|\le|B_1|(\operatorname{diam}\Omega)^{n+1}\int_{\{k<u<l\}}|\nabla u|\), with a ball version and a squared form for \(W^{1,2}\) (`TauCeti.W1p.sub_mul_measureReal_mul_measureReal_le_of_convex`, [De Giorgi](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Poincare/Wirtinger/DeGiorgi.lean)).

**Sobolev inequalities on sets of finite measure and Trudinger's inequality.** Let \(E\) be a real normed space of dimension \(n\ge1\) with an additive Haar measure, \(\omega=|B_1|\), and \(\Omega\) of finite measure. If \(1\le p\le q<\infty\) and \(\delta=1/p-1/q<1/n\), every \(u\in W^{1,p}_0(\Omega)\) satisfies \(\|u\|_q\le n^{-1}\omega^{-1/n}\bigl((1-\delta)/(1/n-\delta)\bigr)^{1-\delta}|\Omega|^{1/n-\delta}\|\nabla u\|_p\), in every dimension and without the restriction \(p<n\) ([finite measure](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/FiniteMeasure.lean)). The proof bounds \(|u|\) by the Riesz potential of \(|\nabla u|\) of order one and uses the estimate \(\|\int_\Omega|x-y|^{n(\kappa-1)}f(y)\,dy\|_{L^q(\Omega)}\le C\,|\Omega|^{\kappa-\delta}\|f\|_{L^p(\Omega)}\) for \(\delta<\kappa\le1\) (`TauCeti.eLpNorm_setLIntegral_enorm_sub_rpow_mul_le`, Gilbarg–Trudinger Lemma 7.12, [Riesz potential](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Integral/RieszPotential.lean)); this is not the whole-space Hardy–Littlewood–Sobolev inequality. In the borderline case \(p=n\) the moment bounds grow like \(q^{1-1/n}\), and summing them gives **Trudinger's inequality**: for \(u\in W^{1,n}_0(\Omega)\) and for compactly supported \(C^1\) functions,
\[
\int_\Omega\exp\Bigl(\bigl(|u|/(c\,\|\nabla u\|_n)\bigr)^{n/(n-1)}\Bigr)\le e\,(n^n+5)\,|\Omega|,\qquad c=(2e)^{1-1/n}n^{-1}\omega^{-1/n}
\]
(`TauCeti.W1p.setLIntegral_exp_abs_value_div_rpow_le`, `TauCeti.setLIntegral_exp_norm_div_rpow_le`, [Trudinger](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Trudinger.lean)). The exponent is explicit, not Moser's sharp \(\alpha_n\).

**Prékopa–Leindler and Brunn–Minkowski.** For a measure on a real module, `Measure.HasPrekopaLeindler θ` states that measurable \(f,g,h\colon E\to[0,\infty]\) with \(f(x)^{1-\theta}g(y)^\theta\le h((1-\theta)x+\theta y)\) for all \(x,y\) satisfy \((\int f)^{1-\theta}(\int g)^\theta\le\int h\). The property tensorizes over products and transfers along measure-preserving maps preserving affine combinations; Lebesgue measure on \(\mathbb R\), on \(\iota\to\mathbb R\), and on every finite-dimensional real inner product space has it for \(0\le\theta\le1\) (`TauCeti.hasPrekopaLeindler_volume`, [Prékopa–Leindler](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Integral/PrekopaLeindler.lean)). Consequently, for nonempty null-measurable \(A,B\) in a positive-dimensional Euclidean space, \(|A|^{1/n}+|B|^{1/n}\le|A+B|^{1/n}\) with outer volume on the right, together with the multiplicative form \(|A|^{1-\theta}|B|^\theta\le|(1-\theta)A+\theta B|\) and real-valued versions (`TauCeti.volume_rpow_add_volume_rpow_le`, [Brunn–Minkowski](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/BrunnMinkowski/Basic.lean); the one-dimensional case in [Real.lean](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/BrunnMinkowski/Real.lean)). For convex bodies \(K,L\), \(t\mapsto|K+tL|^{1/n}\) is concave on \(t\ge0\) and bounded below by \(|K|^{1/n}+t|L|^{1/n}\); equality along the whole profile is characterized when the right derivative at zero is given as a hypothesis ([parallel volume](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/BrunnMinkowski/Parallel.lean)). The equality case of Brunn–Minkowski (homothety) is not proved. The **isodiametric inequality** \(|s|\le\omega_n(\operatorname{diam}s/2)^n\) holds for every bounded set in a finite-dimensional real inner product space, with outer measure and no measurability hypothesis (`TauCeti.volume_le_volume_closedBall_diam_div_two`, [isodiametric](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/Isodiametric.lean)).

**Planar isoperimetry and Wirtinger's inequality.** For an absolutely continuous \(f\colon[a,b]\to V\) into a finite-dimensional real inner product space with \(f(a)=f(b)\) and \(f'\in L^2\), \(\int_a^b|f-\bar f|^2\le((b-a)/2\pi)^2\int_a^b|f'|^2\), with equality exactly for the mean plus a first harmonic (`TauCeti.integral_norm_sub_average_sq_le`, `TauCeti.integral_norm_sub_average_sq_eq_iff`, [Wirtinger](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Wirtinger.lean)). For an absolutely continuous closed plane curve with square-integrable derivative, \(4\pi|\operatorname{signedArea}\gamma|\le(b-a)\int_a^b|\gamma'|^2\), with equality exactly for constant curves and circles traversed once at constant speed; for a curve parametrized proportionally to arclength this is the isoperimetric inequality \(|\text{area}|\le L^2/4\pi\) (`TauCeti.four_pi_mul_abs_signedArea_le`, [area–energy](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/AreaEnergy.lean)).

**Rearrangement, perimeter, eigenvalues, and curvature–dimension.** The Schwarz (symmetric decreasing) rearrangement of an arbitrary \(f\colon E\to[0,\infty]\) is defined from the distribution function; for an additive Haar measure it is measurable, radially nonincreasing, monotone, idempotent, independent of the a.e. representative, equimeasurable with \(f\), and preserves every \(L^p\) norm (`TauCeti.addHaar_setOf_lt_schwarzRearrangement`, `TauCeti.eLpNorm_schwarzRearrangement`, [Schwarz rearrangement](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/SchwarzRearrangement.lean)); no Pólya–Szegő gradient inequality is proved. De Giorgi's perimeter \(P(s;\Omega)\) is defined as the total variation of the indicator, is invariant under null modifications and complements, and is lower semicontinuous under local convergence in measure (`TauCeti.perimeter_le_liminf`, [perimeter](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Perimeter.lean)). For a symmetric coercive divergence-form operator on a nonempty bounded open set, the first Dirichlet eigenvalue is the minimum of the Rayleigh quotient over \(H^1_0(\Omega)\), attained at an eigenfunction (`TauCeti.PDE.isLeast_rayleighQuotient_firstDirichletEigenvalue`, [Dirichlet spectrum](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Spectrum/Basic.lean)). The synthetic condition \(CD(K,\infty)\) of Sturm and Lott–Villani is defined for metric-measure spaces as weak \(K\)-convexity of the Boltzmann entropy on \(P_2(X)\) ([curvature–dimension](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/MetricMeasure/CurvatureDimension.lean)); no functional inequality is derived from it. The Yamabe bubbles \((1+|x|^2)^{-(n-2)/2}\), up to scaling and translation, are defined and shown to solve the critical equation ([bubble](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Yamabe/Bubble.lean)), but their extremality for the Sobolev inequality is not proved.

## Topics of Section 14 not found in either inspected library

- The equality case of the Brunn–Minkowski inequality (homothetic convex bodies). The inequality itself, the Prékopa–Leindler inequality, and the isodiametric inequality are in TauCeti.
- The Euclidean isoperimetric inequality for sets of finite perimeter in dimension \(n\ge2\), its quantitative form with Fraenkel asymmetry, and isoperimetric inequalities on manifolds. TauCeti has De Giorgi perimeter with lower semicontinuity and the planar isoperimetric inequality for closed curves (area against length), but no inequality between perimeter and volume.
- Gaussian isoperimetry. The Gaussian integral and the Fourier transform of a Gaussian are present; they are not an isoperimetric theorem.
- Logarithmic Sobolev, Nash, Hardy, and Hardy–Littlewood–Sobolev inequalities, the Bakry–Émery \(\Gamma_2\)-calculus, and transport proofs of functional inequalities. TauCeti defines the synthetic \(CD(K,\infty)\) condition and proves Riesz-potential bounds on sets of finite measure, but derives no such inequality.
- Spectral inequalities of shape optimization: Faber–Krahn, Szegő–Weinberger, and the Pólya–Szegő inequality. TauCeti has the Schwarz rearrangement with equimeasurability and the first Dirichlet eigenvalue as a Rayleigh minimum.
- Sharp constants and extremizers: the sharp Sobolev inequality (Aubin–Talenti), and Moser's sharp exponent in the Moser–Trudinger inequality. Trudinger's inequality with a non-sharp exponent is in TauCeti.
- Sobolev inequalities on Riemannian manifolds. The Euclidean Gagliardo–Nirenberg–Sobolev inequality above is the Sobolev inequality in this checkout, and it is written out in Section 26.

# Core knowledge in mathematical analysis: synopses

The series `SYNOPSIS04.md`–`SYNOPSIS53.md` describes the mathematical content
of the fifty sub-areas numbered 04–53 in [CKMA-bib.md](CKMA-bib.md). It is
addressed to mathematicians and is independent of the separate library-coverage
inventories.

Section numbers remain stable. For the lettered bibliographical subdivisions,
MSC2020 correspondence, and topics spanning several sections, see the
[MSC2020 and cross-subject index](CKMA-MSC2020.md). The mathematical material
is grouped under those same lettered subdivisions where applicable. Existing
result identifiers are retained after topical moves,
so they can occur out of numerical order; references from the gap inventory
continue to identify the same statements.

Each synopsis gives the field's definitions, conventions, and principal theorems
under lettered subdivisions where present and then thematic headings, followed
by a reference guide. The reference guides identify the source basis of the
selected material; a subdivision heading does not imply that every topic in
its bibliography has a separate numbered theorem inventory. Selection is based on
recurring material in the indicated graduate texts and monographs: a subject's
working core can include material treated in several advanced references even
when it is absent from some introductory courses. The aim is neither the
intersection of all the books nor the union of everything any one of them treats.
Standard usable formulations are preferred to a catalogue of every variant.

The reference guides distinguish the main overlapping treatments and identify
local source copies used. An incomplete local copy is identified explicitly;
having a book's table of contents is not treated as having its full text.
Numbered entries count principal results or closely related theorem families,
not every individual assertion within them. Definitions are interleaved with
the statements that use them. Proofs are outside the scope of these synopses.

## Revision conventions

Preserve outer section numbers and existing result identifiers. Follow the
current lettered subdivisions in the bibliography, and move definitions with
the material that uses them. Result labels are stable identifiers rather than
a requirement for increasing display order. For mathematical revisions and
expansions, read both bibliography entries and compare
substantial treatments in several of the indicated references. Consult local
PDFs where available, distinguishing actual text from previews and incomplete
copies. Use other accessible copies when needed and record the source basis.

A target of roughly 30–100 numbered principal results per section leaves room
for the varying breadth of the areas. State essential hypotheses and normalizations,
including exceptional cases that change the conclusion. Some overlap between
neighboring synopses is useful when it explains how the same theorem functions
in different subjects. Update this index when entry counts or titles change.

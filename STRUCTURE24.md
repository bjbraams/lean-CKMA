## Generators and resolvents

[Mathematical scope](SYNOPSIS24.md#generators-and-resolvents).

### Mathlib

**Supporting operator constructions.**

The power-series exponential of a complete normed algebra over \(\mathbb{R}\) or \(\mathbb{C}\) satisfies \(\exp((s+t)a)=\exp(sa)\exp(ta)\), because \(sa\) and \(ta\) commute (`NormedSpace.exp_add_of_commute`). The orbit \(t\mapsto\exp(ta)\) is therefore a norm-continuous one-parameter group inside the group of units (`Analysis/Normed/Algebra/Exponential.lean`). Section 20 states the exponential. This identity is not a generation theorem for strongly continuous semigroups.

### TauCeti

TauCeti develops strongly continuous semigroups on real Banach spaces (`StronglyContinuousSemigroup`, with strong continuity required only at \(0\)); a complex Banach space is treated as a real one, with complex linearity as an extra hypothesis (`IsComplexLinear`, `complexGenerator`). Every semigroup has a growth bound \(\|S(t)\|\le Me^{\omega t}\) with \(M\ge1\) (`StronglyContinuousSemigroup.existsGrowthBound`), and orbits are jointly continuous in time and vector (`tendsto_realOperator_apply`, `continuousOn_realOperator_apply`) ([growth bounds](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/GrowthBound.lean)). The generator is a partially defined operator (`StronglyContinuousSemigroup.generator`, a `LinearPMap`); the local orbit integral \(\int_0^tS(s)x\,ds\) lies in its domain with image \(S(t)x-x\) (`integral_orbit_mem_domain`, `generator_integral_orbit`), and the domain is dense (`dense_domain`) ([generator](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generator/Basic.lean)). The generator is closed (`isClosed_generator`, [closedness](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generator/Closed.lean)); its domain is invariant under the semigroup, which commutes with it there (`map_mem_domain`, `generator_map`, [invariance](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generator/Invariance.lean)); orbits of domain vectors are differentiable with derivative \(AS(t)x=S(t)Ax\) (right-sided at \(0\)), and domain membership is equivalent to right differentiability at \(0\) ([orbit derivatives](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generator/OrbitDerivative.lean)). Every iterated domain \(D(A^n)\) and the space of smooth vectors are dense, and \(x\in D(A^n)\) exactly when the orbit is \(C^n\) on \([0,\infty)\) ([iterated domains](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generator/IteratedDomain.lean), [smooth vectors](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generator/SmoothVectors.lean), [density of smooth vectors](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generator/SmoothDensity.lean)).

The generator determines the semigroup (`StronglyContinuousSemigroup.eq_of_generator_eq`, [uniqueness](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generator/Uniqueness.lean)). A bounded operator \(A\) generates \(e^{tA}\), and every semigroup with generator \(A\) on the whole space is that one (`ofBounded_generator`, `eq_ofBounded_of_generator_eq`, [bounded generators](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/BoundedGenerator/Basic.lean)); a semigroup is continuous in operator norm at zero exactly when its generator has full domain, that is, is bounded (`continuousAt_zero_iff_domain_eq_top`, [uniformly continuous semigroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/UniformlyContinuous.lean)).

For real \(\lambda\) above the growth exponent, the Laplace transform \(\int_0^\infty e^{-\lambda t}S(t)\,dt\) is the resolvent of the generator (`generator_resolvent_eq`), with \(\|R(\lambda,A)\|\le M/(\lambda-\omega)\) and the resolvent identity ([resolvent identity](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Resolvent/Identity.lean)); the sharp power bounds \(\|R(\lambda,A)^n\|\le M/(\lambda-\omega)^n\) follow from the integral formula for resolvent powers (`norm_generator_resolvent_pow_le`, [power bounds](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Resolvent/PowerBounds.lean)), and \(\lambda\mapsto R(\lambda)\) is smooth with \(R'=-R^2\) ([derivatives](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Resolvent/Deriv.lean)). For complex-linear semigroups the open half-plane \(\mathop{\mathrm{Re}}\lambda>\omega\) lies in the complex resolvent set, the resolvent is the Laplace transform there with \(\|R(\lambda,A)\|\le M/(\mathop{\mathrm{Re}}\lambda-\omega)\), and it is holomorphic (`setOf_lt_re_subset_resolventSet_complexGenerator`, `norm_resolvent_complexGenerator_le`, `analyticOnNhd_resolvent_complexGenerator`, [complex resolvent](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Resolvent/Complex.lean)); a real semigroup reaches this through its complexification, which preserves growth bounds ([complexification](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Complexification.lean), [complexified resolvent](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Resolvent/Complexification.lean)).

The Hille–Yosida theorem is proved as a generation equivalence on real Banach spaces: an operator \(A\) generates a semigroup with growth bound \((\omega,M)\) exactly when \(M\ge1\), \(D(A)\) is dense, every real \(\lambda>\omega\) is in the resolvent set, and \(\|R(\lambda,A)^n\|\le M/(\lambda-\omega)^n\) for \(n\ge1\) (`hilleYosida_generation_iff`, [Hille–Yosida](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generation/HilleYosida/Generation.lean)); closedness is a consequence rather than a hypothesis. Dissipativity is defined by \(\lambda\|x\|\le\|\lambda x-Ax\|\) for \(\lambda>0\), and m-dissipativity adds surjectivity of \(\lambda I-A\) for one \(\lambda>0\), which propagates to all \(\lambda>0\) ([dissipative operators](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Dissipative/Basic.lean)). The Lumer–Phillips theorem states that \(A\) generates a contraction semigroup exactly when it is densely defined and m-dissipative (`exists_contractionSemigroup_generator_eq_iff`), with variants through the duality set (`exists_contractionSemigroup_generator_eq_iff_exists_mem_dualitySet_apply_nonpos`) ([Lumer–Phillips](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generation/LumerPhillips.lean), [duality map](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Dissipative/Duality.lean)). On a real inner product space dissipativity is equivalent to \(\langle Ax,x\rangle\le0\) (`isDissipative_iff_real_inner_nonpos`, [Hilbert criterion](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Dissipative/Hilbert.lean)); for a complex Hilbert space regarded as a real one this is \(\mathop{\mathrm{Re}}\langle Ax,x\rangle\le0\). The criterion through dissipativity of \(A\) and \(A^*\) is not formalized.

The Yosida approximations \(A_\lambda=\lambda AR(\lambda,A)\) of an m-dissipative operator are bounded with contractive exponentials (`norm_exp_smul_yosidaApproximation_le_one`, [Yosida approximation](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generation/Yosida/Basic.lean)), and for a dense domain \(e^{tA_\lambda}x\) converges uniformly on compact time intervals to the semigroup generated by \(A\) (`IsMDissipative.tendstoUniformlyOn_exp_yosidaApproximation`, [Yosida limit](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generation/LimitSemigroup.lean)); the analogous pointwise convergence under the Hille–Yosida bounds is `tendsto_hilleYosidaSemigroup`.

Strongly continuous groups are defined with their growth bounds, generators and two-sided orbit derivatives (`StronglyContinuousGroup`, [groups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Group/Basic.lean), [group generators](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Group/Generator.lean)). If \(A\) and \(-A\) both generate semigroups, their operators at equal times are mutually inverse (`comp_eq_id_of_generator_eq_neg`, [negated generators](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generator/Neg.lean)) and the two semigroups glue into a \(C_0\)-group (`StronglyContinuousSemigroup.toGroupOfInverse`, [gluing](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Group/InverseSemigroups.lean)). Stone's theorem is proved in both directions on complex Hilbert spaces: the complex generator of a unitary group is skew-adjoint (`IsUnitary.complexGenerator_adjoint_eq_neg`, [generator direction](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Group/Unitary.lean)), and a self-adjoint \(A\) has exactly one unitary group with complex generator \(iA\) (`IsSelfAdjoint.existsUnique_isUnitary_complexGenerator_eq_I_smul`, `LinearPMap.isSelfAdjoint_iff_exists_isUnitary_complexGenerator_eq_I_smul`, [converse](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Group/Stone/Unbounded.lean)), using m-dissipativity of \(\pm iA\) ([skew multiples](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Dissipative/SkewSelfAdjoint.lean)); see also Section 22.

Concrete examples are treated. For a nonnegative a.e. measurable multiplier \(m\) on an arbitrary measure space and \(1\le p<\infty\), \(S(t)f=e^{-tm}f\) is a contraction semigroup on \(L^p(\mu;E)\) whose generator is multiplication by \(-m\) on the exact domain \(\{f: mf\in L^p\}\), with resolvent multiplication by \((\lambda+m)^{-1}\) ([multiplication semigroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Multiplication/MeasureLp/Generator.lean)); multipliers bounded below, the sequence spaces \(\ell^p\), and bounded continuous multipliers on bounded continuous functions are also treated ([bounded below](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Multiplication/MeasureLp/BoundedBelow.lean), [\(\ell^p\)](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Multiplication/Lp/Basic.lean), [bounded continuous](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Multiplication/Basic.lean)). On a finite-dimensional real inner product space and \(1\le p<\infty\), convolution with the Gaussian heat kernel is a strongly continuous contraction semigroup \(e^{t\Delta}\) on \(L^p\) with Banach-space values, and on \(C^2\) functions with compact support its generator is the Laplacian; the full generator domain is not determined ([heat semigroup](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/HeatKernel/Semigroup.lean), [generator](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/HeatKernel/Generator.lean)).

**Synopsis entries.** Covered: 24.1–24.7, 24.9, 24.11 (TauCeti). Partly covered: 24.8 (the inner-product characterization of dissipativity; not the criterion through \(A\) and \(A^*\)). Not covered: 24.10.

## Cauchy problems and perturbations

[Mathematical scope](SYNOPSIS24.md#cauchy-problems-and-perturbations).

### Mathlib

**Supporting operator constructions.**

Ordinary differential equations, including the Picard–Lindelöf theorem, are Section 31 (`Analysis/ODE/PicardLindelof.lean`).

### TauCeti

Classical and mild solutions of \(u'=Au\), \(u(0)=x\), are defined (`IsClassicalSolution`, `IsMildSolution`, the latter by \(A\int_0^tu=u(t)-x\)). Semigroup orbits are mild solutions for every \(x\) and classical solutions for \(x\in D(A)\), and every classical or mild solution equals the orbit on \([0,\infty)\) (`isClassicalSolution_realOperator`, `isMildSolution_realOperator`, `IsClassicalSolution.unique`, `IsMildSolution.unique`) ([solutions](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/CauchyProblem/Basic.lean), [uniqueness](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/CauchyProblem/Uniqueness.lean)). The converse, that well-posedness of the Cauchy problem forces generation, is not formalized. The inhomogeneous problem is treated only for a bounded operator \(B\) and continuous forcing: \(u'=Bu+f\) has a unique global solution for every initial value, given by the integrating-factor formula (`ContinuousLinearMap.existsUnique_hasDerivAt_add`, [inhomogeneous equations](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/ODE/Inhomogeneous.lean)).

The bounded perturbation theorem is proved with the sharp exponent: if \(S\) has growth bound \((\omega,M)\) and \(B\) is bounded, then \(B+A\) on \(D(A)\) generates a semigroup with growth bound \((\omega+M\|B\|,M)\) (`StronglyContinuousSemigroup.exists_generator_eq_vadd`, [perturbation](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generation/BoundedPerturbation.lean)); the perturbed resolvent is located in \((\omega+M\|B\|,\infty)\) ([perturbed resolvent](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Resolvent/Perturbation.lean)). TauCeti's Trotter formula ([Lie groups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/Exponential/Trotter.lean)) concerns exponential curves in finite-dimensional Lie groups, not unbounded generators.

**Synopsis entries.** Covered: 24.14 (TauCeti). Partly covered: 24.12 (orbits are the unique classical and mild solutions; the converse well-posedness characterization is absent), 24.13 (bounded generator and continuous forcing only). Not covered: 24.15–24.19.

## Analyticity, smoothing, and fractional powers

[Mathematical scope](SYNOPSIS24.md#analyticity-smoothing-and-fractional-powers).

### Mathlib

**Supporting operator constructions.**

For a positive element \(a\) of a C⋆-algebra and \(0<p<1\), there is a measure \(\mu\) on \(\mathbb R\) with \(a^p=\int_{t>0} t^p\bigl(t^{-1}-(t+a)^{-1}\bigr)\,d\mu(t)\), the integrand taken in the continuous functional calculus; an analogous representation holds for \(1<p<2\) (`CFC.exists_measure_nnrpow_eq_integral_cfcₙ_rpowIntegrand₀₁` and `…₁₂` in `Analysis/SpecialFunctions/ContinuousFunctionalCalculus/Rpow/IntegralRepresentation.lean`). The measure is determined only up to a constant factor; the representation is used to prove operator monotonicity and concavity of \(a\mapsto a^p\) for \(0\le p\le1\) (`CFC.monotone_rpow`, `CFC.concaveOn_rpow` in `Rpow/Order.lean`). This is the C⋆-algebraic shadow of the Balakrishnan formula, not a fractional-power theory for sectorial operators on Banach spaces.

### TauCeti

**Supporting material.** The heat semigroup is ultracontractive, \(\|e^{t\Delta}f\|_\infty\le(4\pi t)^{-n/(2p)}\|f\|_p\), and has smooth representatives at positive times ([smoothing](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/HeatKernel/Smoothing.lean)); this is a concrete smoothing estimate, not the abstract analytic-semigroup theory.

**Synopsis entries.** Not covered: 24.20–24.27.

## Holomorphic calculus and maximal regularity

[Mathematical scope](SYNOPSIS24.md#holomorphic-calculus-and-maximal-regularity).

### Mathlib

The continuous functional calculus of a normal or self-adjoint element of a C⋆-algebra is the star-isometric equivalence of Sections 21 and 23, from continuous functions on the spectrum onto the closed star-subalgebra generated by the element (`Analysis/CStarAlgebra/ContinuousFunctionalCalculus/Basic.lean`). ## Form methods, products, and subordination

[Mathematical scope](SYNOPSIS24.md#form-methods-products-and-subordination).

### TauCeti

The J.-L. Lions theorem is proved for real Hilbert spaces. For a continuous linear \(\iota:V\to H\) from a real Hilbert space into a real inner product space (a Gelfand triple when \(\iota\) is injective with dense range), a weakly measurable, essentially bounded family \(A(t):V\to V'\) satisfying Gårding's inequality \(\alpha\|v\|^2-\beta\|\iota v\|^2\le A(t)vv\), \(f\in L^2(a,b;V')\) and \(u_0\in H\), the problem \(u'+A(t)u=f\), \(u(a)=u_0\), has a weak solution \(u\in L^2(a,b;V)\) with \(u'\in L^2(a,b;V')\) when \(V\) is separable (`exists_hasWeakLineDerivOn_gelfandDual`, [existence](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Parabolic/Existence.lean)); \(\iota\circ u\) has a representative continuous on \([a,b]\), and the energy estimate gives uniqueness (`HasWeakLineDerivOn.eqOn_of_gelfandDual`, [energy estimate](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Parabolic/EnergyEstimate.lean)). Section 33 records the application to divergence-form parabolic equations ([Dirichlet problem](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Parabolic/DirichletProblem.lean)).

For subordination only the scalar side is present. Bernstein functions are defined; for a Bernstein function \(f\), \(t\mapsto e^{-xf(t)}\) is completely monotone for every \(x\ge0\), and this characterizes Bernstein functions (`IsBernsteinFunction.isContinuousCompletelyMonotoneOnIoi_exp_neg_mul`, `isBernsteinFunction_iff_nonneg_and_forall_isContinuousCompletelyMonotoneOnIoi_exp_neg_mul`, [composition](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/CompletelyMonotone/Composition.lean), [exponents](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/CompletelyMonotone/Bernstein/Exponential/Basic.lean)); Bernstein's theorem represents a completely monotone function as a Laplace transform (`exists_representsLaplace_of_isCompletelyMonotone`, [Bernstein's theorem](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/CompletelyMonotone/Bernstein/Theorem.lean)); \(t\mapsto t^\alpha\), \(0\le\alpha\le1\), is a Bernstein function (`isBernsteinFunction_rpow`, [powers](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/CompletelyMonotone/Bernstein/Power.lean)); and Bernstein functions have a Lévy–Khintchine representation ([Lévy–Khintchine](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/CompletelyMonotone/Bernstein/LevyKhintchine/Representation.lean)). The convolution-semigroup property of the representing measures and the subordinated operator semigroup are not formalized.

**Synopsis entries.** Partly covered: 24.49 (real Hilbert spaces; existence needs \(V\) separable), 24.50 (scalar part only: \(e^{-tf}\) is completely monotone, hence a Laplace transform; no convolution semigroup or subordinated operator semigroup). Not covered: 24.45–24.48.

## Nonlinear, nonautonomous, and Volterra evolution

[Mathematical scope](SYNOPSIS24.md#nonlinear-nonautonomous-and-volterra-evolution).

### Mathlib

**Supporting material.** The Picard–Lindelöf theorem gives local existence and uniqueness for Lipschitz vector fields on complete normed spaces (`Analysis/ODE/PicardLindelof.lean`); this is the case \(A=0\) of semilinear well-posedness.

### TauCeti

For a globally Lipschitz vector field on a real Banach space there is a unique solution defined on all of \(\mathbb R\) (`ODE.globalSolution`, `eq_globalSolution`, [global solutions](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/ODE/GlobalSolution.lean)); with \(F\) globally Lipschitz and \(A\) bounded this is the global case of 24.55.

Gradient flows are developed in the metric formulation of Ambrosio–Gigli–Savaré, not through m-accretive operators. For a lower semicontinuous energy \(\varphi\) on a metric space, solutions of the evolution variational inequality \(\mathrm{EVI}_\lambda\) are \(\lambda\)-contracting and determined by their initial point, and define a continuous semiflow on the set of initial points from which a solution starts (`IsEVISolution`, `eviFlow_add`, `dist_eviFlow_le`, [EVI](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/GradientFlow/EVI/Basic.lean), [EVI semigroup](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/GradientFlow/EVI/Semigroup.lean)); existence of solutions from every point of \(\overline{D(\varphi)}\) is not proved. Along an \(\mathrm{EVI}_\lambda\) solution the energy identity holds, with \(\frac d{dt}\varphi(u(t))=-|\partial\varphi|^2(u(t))\) and \(|u'|(t)=|\partial\varphi|(u(t))\) for almost every \(t>0\) (`IsEVISolution.apply_eq_add_dissipation`, `IsEVISolution.ae_descendingSlope_eq_metricDerivative`, [EVI and maximal slope](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/GradientFlow/EVI/MaximalSlope.lean)). The Moreau–Yosida approximation, the metric resolvent \(J_\tau\) and minimizing movements are also formalized ([Moreau–Yosida](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/GradientFlow/MoreauYosida/Basic.lean), [minimizing movements](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/GradientFlow/MinimizingMovement.lean)). The convex subdifferential is defined and shown to be monotone, without maximal monotonicity ([subdifferential](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Subdifferential.lean)).

**Synopsis entries.** Partly covered: 24.53 (metric \(\mathrm{EVI}_\lambda\) formulation: uniqueness, contraction and semigroup law, without existence or the subdifferential formulation), 24.54 (energy identity for \(\mathrm{EVI}_\lambda\) solutions in metric form), 24.55 (only bounded \(A\) with globally Lipschitz \(F\), and the local Lipschitz case for \(A=0\)). Not covered: 24.51, 24.52, 24.56, 24.64, 24.57.

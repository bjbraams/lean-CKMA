## Holomorphic maps, coverings, and uniformization

[Mathematical scope](SYNOPSIS44.md#holomorphic-maps-coverings-and-uniformization).

### Mathlib

**Complex-manifold foundations.**

The general manifold framework supports complex atlases and holomorphic maps. A boundaryless manifold modeled on \(\mathbb C\), with complex-smooth transition maps, provides the atlas structure underlying a Riemann surface. Hausdorffness and second countability are separate hypotheses, as in the general manifold library; there is no dedicated global theory of Riemann surfaces.

The upper half-plane is a one-dimensional complex manifold via its inclusion in \(\mathbb C\). Möbius transformations of positive determinant act holomorphically (`Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/Manifold.lean`). The action of \(\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)\) on \(\mathbb H\) is proper (`Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/ProperAction.lean`), and arithmetic subgroups of \(\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb R)\) act properly discontinuously (`NumberTheory/ModularForms/ProperlyDiscontinuous.lean`); this is supporting material for quotient surfaces, not uniformization. Its hyperbolic metric and invariant measure are described in Section 40; infinitesimal conformality is Section 43.

The maximum principle is proved on boundaryless complex manifolds, including constancy of holomorphic functions into a complex normed space on a connected compact complex manifold (`MDifferentiable.apply_eq_of_compactSpace` and `MDifferentiable.exists_eq_const_of_compactSpace` in `Geometry/Manifold/Complex.lean`; see Section 45). On the upper half-plane itself, a holomorphic function vanishing frequently near a point vanishes identically (`UpperHalfPlane.eq_zero_of_frequently`).

**Plane coverings and steps toward the Riemann mapping theorem.**

`Analysis/Complex/CoveringMap.lean` proves that \(\exp:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C\setminus\{0\}\) is a covering map, a quotient by \(2\pi i\mathbb Z\). Over a proper nontrivially normed field such as \(\mathbb C\), the power maps \(z\mapsto z^n\) with \(n\neq0\) in the field are covering maps of the punctured field, and a polynomial is a covering map over the complement of its critical values (`Polynomial.isCoveringMapOn_eval`).

`Analysis/Complex/RiemannMapping.lean` contains only the first two steps of the Riemann mapping theorem: an open simply connected \(U\subsetneq\mathbb C\) admits a map that is injective on \(U\), sends \(U\) into the open unit disc, and has nonvanishing derivative there (`Complex.exists_mapsTo_unitBall_injOn_deriv_ne_zero`). The theorem itself is not in Mathlib.

### TauCeti

For holomorphic maps between Riemann surfaces (complex manifolds modeled on \(\mathbb C\)), local multiplicity is defined independently of charts and proved multiplicative under composition; multiplicity one characterizes local injectivity, and ramification points of a map that is nonconstant near a point are isolated ([multiplicity](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/LocalMultiplicity.lean)). A map holomorphic and nonconstant near a point has the local form \(u\mapsto u^m\) in suitable centered charts, with \(m\) the local multiplicity (`TauCeti.RiemannSurface.isLocalPowerAt_localMultiplicity`, [local normal form](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/LocalNormalForm.lean)). The identity theorem and the open mapping theorem hold on Riemann surfaces ([identity theorem](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/IdentityTheorem.lean), [open mapping](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/OpenMapping.lean)).

For a map with compact source that is constant near no point, the number of preimages counted with multiplicity is locally constant, so over a connected target it defines the degree; the degree is multiplicative under composition, and such a map from a nonempty compact surface onto a connected surface is surjective (`TauCeti.RiemannSurface.surjective_of_forall_not_eventuallyConst`, [degree](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/Degree.lean)); between compact connected Riemann surfaces every nonconstant holomorphic map is a finite holomorphic map, and a map of degree one is a biholomorphism. Divisor pullback multiplies degrees by the degree of the map ([pullback](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/Divisor.lean)). The ramification divisor \(R_f=\sum_x(e_x-1)[x]\) of a finite holomorphic map is defined, it is finite on a compact source, the fibre over \(y\) has \(\deg f-\sum_{x\in f^{-1}(y)}(e_x-1)\) points, and the chain rule \(R_{g\circ f}=R_f+f^*R_g\) holds ([ramification](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/Ramification.lean)). With compact source the map is a covering exactly over the complement of the finite set of branch values, with every unbranched fibre of cardinality the degree ([branch values](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/BranchValues.lean)). The file docstrings state that the Euler-characteristic step of Riemann–Hurwitz is not part of this development. Meromorphic orders on a Riemann surface are chart independent and multiply by the local multiplicity under pullback ([meromorphic orders](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/Meromorphic.lean)). The power maps of the Riemann sphere `OnePoint ℂ` are finite holomorphic maps of degree \(n\) branched only over \(0\) and \(\infty\) ([power maps](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/PowerMap.lean)).

Analytic Belyi pairs (compact surfaces with a map to the sphere branched only over \(0,1,\infty\)) are coverings over the thrice-punctured sphere with well-defined monodromy triples ([Belyi pairs](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/BelyiPair.lean)), and connected covers of \(\mathbb C\setminus\{0,1\}\) are classified by connected permutation triples ([classification](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ThricePuncturedSphere/Classification.lean)). The genus formula for the associated filled covers is combinatorial, not the genus of a compact Riemann surface ([filled cover](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ThricePuncturedSphere/FilledCover/Ramification.lean)).

TauCeti develops concrete Riemann surfaces from discrete subgroups of \(\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb R)\): coarse upper-half-plane quotients and their cusp extensions have complex-manifold atlases, including the cusp charts ([cusp-extended quotient](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/Manifold.lean)). Despite the type name `CompactifiedQuotient`, compactness is a further theorem (`compactSpace_of_compact_truncations`) under a compact-truncation condition and finiteness of cusp orbits; it should not be asserted for every discrete subgroup ([compactness criterion](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/Compactness.lean)). For a finite-index inclusion \(\Delta\le\Gamma\), the induced map of compactified quotients has degree \([\Gamma:\Delta]\), with cusp multiplicities given by stabilizer indices ([degree](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/Degree.lean)); for the modular group, the normalized \(j\)-invariant is a biholomorphism of the compactified level-one quotient onto the Riemann sphere (`jBiholomorph`, [level one](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/LevelOne/Sphere.lean)). These are constructions of particular quotient surfaces; uniformization of an abstract surface is not proved.

The Riemann mapping theorem is proved: every simply connected open proper subset of \(\mathbb C\) is biholomorphic to the unit disc (`riemannMapping`), via an extremal problem (Montel and Hurwitz) and the Koebe square-root step ([existence](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/RiemannMapping/Existence.lean), [Koebe step](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/Koebe.lean)). TauCeti also characterizes holomorphic functions with equal pre-Schwarzian derivative \(f''/f'\) as differing by an affine postcomposition ([pre-Schwarzian](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/PreSchwarzian.lean)). The holomorphic-germ sheaf on the plane and monodromy are also implemented (Sections 40 and 45).

On the Gauss–Bonnet side, the invariant area of a compact convex hyperbolic polygon with \(n\) vertices is \((n-2)\pi-\sum\alpha_i\) (`CompactConvexPolygon.volume_carrier`, [polygon Gauss–Bonnet](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/Polygon/GaussBonnet.lean)). Every discrete subgroup of \(\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb R)\) has a measurable fundamental domain, and its covolume is the hyperbolic area of the quotient ([covolume](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Covolume.lean)); for a side-paired convex polygon satisfying the tessellation hypotheses of Poincaré's theorem, the covolume is \((n-2)\pi-\sum\alpha_i\) (`ConvexPolygon.SidePairing.covolume_eq`, [fundamental polygons](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/FundamentalPolygon.lean)), and it is expressed through the elliptic orbits in [Polygon/Area.lean](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Polygon/Area.lean). The identification of the polygon count with \(2g-2+s\) is explicitly not proved there, and there is no Gauss–Bonnet theorem for a smooth metric on a compact surface.

The Schwarz–Pick theorem is proved for holomorphic self-maps of the unit disc with its Poincaré distance (`TauCeti.hyperbolicDist_map_le`, [hyperbolic distance](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/Hyperbolic/Distance.lean)), with the rigidity case that equality at one pair of distinct points forces a disc automorphism ([Poincaré disc form](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/Poincare/SchwarzPick.lean)). Its extension to maps between hyperbolic surfaces is not present.

## Divisors, line bundles, and Riemann–Roch

[Mathematical scope](SYNOPSIS44.md#divisors-line-bundles-and-riemannroch).

### TauCeti

The statements of this section are proved in TauCeti for algebraic function fields of one variable and for proper curves over a field, not for compact Riemann surfaces; the comparison theorem (44.18) that would transfer them is absent. In this setting they are, however, extensive.

TauCeti proves the Hurwitz genus formula \(2g'-2=n\,(2g-2)+\deg\mathop{\mathrm{Diff}}(F'/F)\) for finite separable extensions of algebraic function fields of one variable with exact constant fields (`hurwitz_genus_formula`, [Hurwitz formula](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Different/Hurwitz.lean)), and the Riemann–Roch theorem for such function fields in the form that the divisor of any nonzero Weil differential is a Riemann–Roch divisor for the genus, with \(\deg W=2g-2\) and \(\ell(W)=g\) (`degree_weilDifferentialDivisor`, `dim_weilDifferentialDivisor`, [Riemann–Roch](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Differential/CanonicalDivisor.lean)). Principal divisors have degree zero, degree descends to the divisor class group, and divisors of negative degree have \(\ell(D)=0\) (`TauCeti.Divisor.degree_principal`, `TauCeti.Divisor.dim_eq_zero_of_degree_neg`, [product formula](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Divisor/ProductFormula.lean)). If \(\deg D>2g-2\) then \(\ell(D)=\deg D+1-g\) ([high degree](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Consequences/HighDegree.lean)). A genus-zero function field with a divisor of degree one is rational (`TauCeti.nonempty_algEquiv_ratFunc_of_genus_eq_zero_of_divisor_degree_eq_one`, [genus zero](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Consequences/GenusZero.lean)). The residues of a Kähler differential of a function field over an algebraically closed constant field sum to zero (`TauCeti.finsum_kaehlerResidueOfPerfectField_eq_zero`; with trace residues over any perfect exact constant field, `TauCeti.finsum_traceResidue_kaehlerBasisOfSeparating_coord_eq_zero`, [residue theorem](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Differential/Independence.lean)).

For line bundles \(L\) on proper curves over a field (integral, separated, Noetherian, with exact constants), Serre duality \(H^1(X,L)^\vee\simeq H^0(X,\omega\otimes L^{-1})\) is proved as the existence of an isomorphism, together with \(h^0(L)-h^0(\omega\otimes L^{-1})=\deg L+1-g\) ([Serre duality](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/LineBundle/SerreDuality.lean), [Euler-characteristic form](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/Scheme/RiemannRoch/Basic.lean)); line bundles of degree greater than \(2g-2\) have \(H^1=0\) ([high degree](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/LineBundle/HighDegree.lean)). Isomorphism classes of line bundles form the Picard group under tensor product ([classes](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/LineBundle/Class.lean)).

At a rational place of a function field of genus \(g\geq1\) with exact constants there are exactly \(g\) Weierstrass gaps, all in \(\{1,\ldots,2g-1\}\) (`TauCeti.Place.card_weierstrassGaps`, [gap theorem](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Consequences/WeierstrassGaps.lean)). Over an algebraically closed constant field in which the gaps at every place are distinct (automatic in characteristic zero), the Weierstrass weights sum to \(g^3-g\) (`TauCeti.finsum_weierstrassWeight`, [total weight](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Differential/Weierstrass/TotalWeight.lean)), and for \(g\geq2\) the number of Weierstrass points lies between \(2g+2\) and \(g^3-g\), with \(2g+2\) exactly in the hyperelliptic case (`TauCeti.isHyperellipticFunctionField_iff_ncard_setOf_weierstrassWeight_ne_zero_eq`, [count](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Differential/Weierstrass/Count.lean)). A hyperelliptic field over an algebraically closed field of characteristic not two has exactly \(2g+2\) branch places over its rational subfield (`TauCeti.card_branchPlaces`, [branch places](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Hyperelliptic/BranchPlaces.lean)), and every genus-two function field with \(2\neq0\) is hyperelliptic (`isHyperellipticFunctionField_of_genus_eq_two`, [hyperelliptic fields](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Hyperelliptic/Basic.lean)).

Its Abel–Jacobi map is a formal divisor-class map into an abstract \(\mathrm{Pic}^0\), without periods or a Jacobian variety ([Abel–Jacobi](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/AbelJacobi/Basic.lean)).

## Moduli, mapping classes, and hyperbolic geometry

[Mathematical scope](SYNOPSIS44.md#moduli-mapping-classes-and-hyperbolic-geometry).

### TauCeti

The Hurwitz automorphism bound is proved for algebraic function fields: a finite group of automorphisms of a function field of genus \(g\geq2\), tame over its fixed field, has order at most \(84(g-1)\); in characteristic zero the tameness hypothesis is dropped, and the full automorphism group, once finite, satisfies the bound (`TauCeti.natCard_le_eighty_four_mul_genus_sub_one_of_charZero`, `TauCeti.card_algEquiv_le_eighty_four_mul_genus_sub_one_of_charZero`, [Hurwitz bound](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Automorphism/HurwitzBound.lean)). The proof is the algebraic Hurwitz formula; the transfer to conformal automorphisms of a compact Riemann surface is not made.

**Synopsis entries.** Partly covered: 44.70 (algebraic function-field version only). Not covered: 44.68, 44.69, 44.71–44.77, 44.80.

## What is present nearby

The Fredholm alternative for a compact operator on a Banach space is section 21. If \(T\) is compact and \(\mu\neq 0\), then \(\mu\) is an eigenvalue or \(\mu\) is in the resolvent set (`IsCompactOperator.hasEigenvalue_or_mem_resolventSet` in `Analysis/Normed/Operator/Compact/FredholmAlternative.lean`; the older module `Analysis/Normed/Operator/FredholmAlternative.lean` is deprecated).

Fredholm operators between Hausdorff topological vector spaces over a complete nontrivially normed field are defined as strict maps with closed finite-codimensional range and complemented finite-dimensional kernel (`ContinuousLinearMap.IsFredholm` in `Analysis/Normed/Operator/Fredholm/Basic.lean`). Between Banach spaces the Fredholm operators form an open set on which the index is locally constant (`isOpen_setOfPred_isFredholm`, `index_continuousOn_isFredholm` in `Analysis/Normed/Operator/Fredholm/Open.lean`). That \(I-K\) is Fredholm of index zero for compact \(K\) is not stated there.

Vector-valued reproducing kernel Hilbert spaces are defined, the kernel of such a space is positive semidefinite, and conversely every positive semidefinite kernel is the kernel of a constructed RKHS (`RKHS.kernel_ofKernel`, essentially Moore's theorem), in `Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Reproducing.lean`. The Lax–Milgram theorem for a bounded coercive bilinear form on a real Hilbert space is in `Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LaxMilgram.lean`.

The integral equation attached to a Lipschitz vector field is the integral form of an ordinary differential equation. Existence and uniqueness for that equation are the Picard–Lindelöf theorem, in section 31 (`Analysis/ODE/PicardLindelof.lean`, `Analysis/ODE/ExistUnique.lean`).

## Additional coverage in TauCeti

The following additions were checked in TauCeti revision `6ebb29f84b4d`; see the [revision, build evidence, and comparison scope](TauCetiCoverage.md). Source links below are pinned to that revision.

Schur's test is proved for a measurable nonnegative kernel whose row integrals are bounded by \(A\) and column integrals by \(B\): for finite \(1\le p\), its integral operator has the \(L^p\) estimate with constant \(A^{1-1/p}B^{1/p}\). More generally, for \(1\le p\le q\) and \(1/p+1/r=1+1/q\), the same bounds on the row and column integrals of \(k^r\) give an \(L^p\to L^q\) bound with constant \(A^{1-1/p}B^{1/q}\) (Young's inequality for integral operators, applicable to weakly singular kernels on bounded sets). The formal statements are nonnegative extended-integral inequalities, with the bounds required almost everywhere ([Schur's test](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Integral/SchurTest.lean)).

For compact operators on Banach spaces, TauCeti proves the finite-dimensional kernel/cokernel and closed-range assertions for \(I-K\) ([Riesz theory](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Compact/RieszTheory.lean)). For a bounded coercive form \(B\) on a real Hilbert space \(V\) and a compact map \(J:V\to H\) into an inner-product space, the Lax–Milgram operator \(K\) with \(B(Ku,v)=\langle Ju,Jv\rangle\) is compact, and \(I-\kappa K\) satisfies the Fredholm alternative with finite-dimensional kernel ([variational Fredholm alternative](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Variational/Fredholm.lean)); the application to the Dirichlet problem on bounded domains is in Section 32. The abstract Neumann series \(R(\mu)=\sum_n(\lambda-\mu)^nR(\lambda)^{n+1}\) for \(|\mu-\lambda|\,\|R(\lambda)\|<1\) is proved for partial operators on Banach spaces ([Neumann series](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Resolvent/NeumannSeries.lean)).

The Hilbert–Schmidt norm of a bounded operator between Hilbert spaces is defined by \(\sum_i\|Tb_i\|^2\) in a Hilbert basis; it is basis-independent, invariant under adjoints, dominates the operator norm, satisfies \(\|ATB\|_{HS}\le\|A\|\,\|T\|_{HS}\|B\|\), and is invariant under left composition with isometries and right composition with coisometries ([Hilbert–Schmidt norm](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/HilbertSchmidt/Basic.lean), [isometric invariance](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/HilbertSchmidt/Isometry.lean)). Operators of finite Hilbert–Schmidt norm correspond to \(\ell^2\) families of columns and are compact ([columns and compactness](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/HilbertSchmidt/Columns.lean)). For a continuous kernel \(k\) on a compact group, the convolution operator \(f\mapsto\int k(xy^{-1})f(y)\,dy\) maps \(L^2(G)\) boundedly into \(C(G)\), is compact on \(L^2(G)\), and is self-adjoint for symmetric kernels ([convolution operators](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Compact/Convolution.lean)). The primitive operator \(f\mapsto\int_a^tf\) on continuous paths over a compact interval is a bounded operator of norm at most \(b-a\) ([integration operator](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/ContinuousMap.lean)); it is the Volterra operator with kernel \(1\).

A theorem identifying the Hilbert–Schmidt norm of an integral operator with the \(L^2\) norm of its kernel, a Volterra resolvent-kernel theory for general kernels, or a complex Fredholm determinant of an integral operator was not located. TauCeti's Fredholm characteristic power series of infinite matrices over complete ultrametric rings concerns Serre's nonarchimedean theory ([source](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/CharPowerSeries/Basic.lean)).

## Topics of Section 25 not found in either inspected library

- The kernel side of Hilbert–Schmidt theory: for \(k\in L^2(D\times D)\) the integral operator is Hilbert–Schmidt with \(\|K\|_{S^2}=\|k\|_{L^2}\) and adjoint kernel \(\overline{k(y,x)}\). The abstract Hilbert–Schmidt norm and compactness are in TauCeti.
- Mercer's theorem: eigenfunction expansion and trace formula for continuous positive definite kernels. Mathlib has RKHS and Moore's theorem; TauCeti has positive-definite functions and kernels.
- Volterra integral equations with general continuous kernels and resolvent kernels (invertibility of \(I-\lambda K\) for all \(\lambda\)), Riemann–Liouville fractional integrals and Abel inversion. TauCeti has only the primitive operator, and the Gronwall inequality, which bounds solutions of Volterra-type integral inequalities, is proved in Mathlib (`Analysis/ODE/Gronwall.lean`, with a discrete form in `Analysis/ODE/DiscreteGronwall.lean`).
- Fredholm integral equations of the first or second kind as a theory of concrete kernel operators, beyond the abstract Fredholm alternative and Riesz theory, and the Fredholm determinant of an integral operator.
- Nyström methods, collectively compact operator approximation, and any other numerical method for integral equations.
- Tikhonov regularization of ill-posed equations: identification of the minimizer of \(\|Ku-f^\delta\|^2+\alpha\|u\|^2\) as \((K^*K+\alpha I)^{-1}K^*f^\delta\).
- Singular integral equations with Cauchy kernel, the Plemelj–Sokhotski formulas, the Riemann–Hilbert boundary problems of Muskhelishvili and Gakhov, and boundary integral operators (layer potentials, their Sobolev mapping properties, the Calderón projector) for elliptic boundary value problems. Cauchy principal values of contour integrals are present in TauCeti (Section 40), but not as a singular-integral-operator theory.

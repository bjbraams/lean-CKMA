## Topics of Section 9 not found in either inspected library

- Malliavin calculus: the Malliavin derivative and its closability, the Sobolev spaces \(\mathbb D^{k,p}\), the Skorokhod integral, the integration-by-parts formula on Wiener space, the Clark–Ocone formula, and smooth-density criteria including Hörmander's theorem.
- Infinite-dimensional Gaussian analysis in the sense of Bogachev and Da Prato: the Cameron–Martin space and theorem, the Wiener chaos decomposition beyond finitely many coordinates, and the Ornstein–Uhlenbeck semigroup and Mehler's formula. Gaussian measures on Banach spaces and Fernique's theorem are in Mathlib (Section 6).
- Rough paths, controlled paths, the sewing lemma, the rough integral, and rough differential equations, including Young integration as used in that theory.
- Regularity structures and paracontrolled distributions.
- Stochastic partial differential equations, their well-posedness, and renormalisation: Hilbert-space stochastic integration against a cylindrical Wiener process, Walsh integration against space-time white noise, Lipschitz mild solutions, and Dalang's condition. Only deterministic mild solutions of linear semigroup Cauchy problems exist (TauCeti).

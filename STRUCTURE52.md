## Geodesics, curvature comparison, and compactness

[Mathematical scope](SYNOPSIS52.md#geodesics-curvature-comparison-and-compactness).

### Mathlib

**Covariant derivatives and the Levi-Civita connection.** A covariant derivative on a vector bundle is an operator on differentiable sections that is additive and satisfies the Leibniz rule. The difference of two connections is a one-form with values in endomorphisms, and adding such a one-form produces another connection. Metric compatibility of a connection on the tangent bundle means that the derivative of the metric tensor along any vector field, corrected by the two terms in which the connection hits one argument of the metric, vanishes. The torsion vanishes if and only if \(\nabla_X Y-\nabla_Y X=[X,Y]\) for differentiable vector fields (`Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/`). The tensoriality criterion turns an operation on sections that is tensorial at a point into a continuous linear or bilinear map on the fibre there (`Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Tensoriality.lean`).

On a finite-dimensional real \(C^2\) manifold whose tangent spaces carry a Riemannian bundle metric of class \(C^1\), a Levi-Civita connection is a covariant derivative that is metric-compatible and torsion-free. The Koszul formula expresses \(\langle\nabla_X Y,Z\rangle\) in terms of derivatives of the metric coefficients and Lie brackets, for vector fields differentiable at the point in question. Two Levi-Civita connections therefore agree on every differentiable vector field, in every tangent direction (`CovariantDerivative.IsLeviCivitaConnection.uniqueness`); the value on a vector field that fails to be differentiable at the point is not constrained by the definition. A Levi-Civita connection is constructed, by tensoriality and the musical isomorphism rather than local frames, and proved to be metric-compatible and torsion-free (`CovariantDerivative.leviCivitaConnection`, `Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/LeviCivita.lean`). The module leaves its smoothness, for metrics of higher regularity, to future work, and a TODO in `CovariantDerivative/Metric.lean` records that parallel transport is not yet available.

**Riemannian length.** Where each tangent space carries an extended norm, the length of a path \(\gamma:\mathbb{R}\to M\) from time \(a\) to time \(b\) is the extended integral over \([a,b]\) of the extended norm of `mfderiv` \(\gamma\) applied to \(1\). The length is invariant under monotone reparametrisation. The Riemannian extended distance is the infimum of the lengths of \(C^1\) paths joining the two points; the file notes that the same quantity would be a Finsler distance, because the norm need not come from an inner product. The triangle inequality is proved (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PathELength.lean`). `IsRiemannianManifold` is the proposition that an already given extended distance coincides with this infimum. An inner-product space, with the constant metric given by its inner product, satisfies the proposition. From a Riemannian metric one can build the extended distance: on a regular space the construction is a pseudo-extended-metric, and on a \(T_3\) space it is an extended metric, and the predicate then holds (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Basic.lean`).

**Supporting material: manifold foundations.** A charted space carries an atlas of open partial homeomorphisms into the model space; a model with corners embeds that model in a normed space, and a \(C^n\) manifold is one whose changes of chart are \(C^n\), for \(n\) a finite extended natural number, \(\infty\), or the analytic regularity \(\omega\) (`Geometry/Manifold/IsManifold/Basic.lean`). Neither the Hausdorff property nor second countability is assumed; those hypotheses are added where a theorem needs them. Maps are \(C^n\) in charts (`Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiff/Defs.lean`), and `mfderiv` is the manifold derivative, set to zero where the map is not differentiable (`Geometry/Manifold/MFDeriv/Defs.lean`). If \(M\) is \(C^{n+1}\), the tangent bundle is a \(C^n\) vector bundle (`Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Tangent.lean`). The Lie bracket of vector fields is the pullback of the bracket in the model space, and satisfies the Jacobi identity (`Geometry/Manifold/VectorField/LieBracket.lean`).

Immersions and submersions are defined by local normal forms appropriate to infinite-dimensional models; the comparison with injectivity or surjectivity of `mfderiv` is described as future work (`Geometry/Manifold/Immersion.lean`, `Geometry/Manifold/Submersion.lean`). A separate notion asks that `mfderiv` have a continuous left inverse; it is implied by injectivity when the target is finite-dimensional (`Geometry/Manifold/ImmersionDiff.lean`). Local diffeomorphisms are local homeomorphisms with open image, and bijective ones are diffeomorphisms (`Geometry/Manifold/LocalDiffeomorph.lean`). A smooth embedding is a \(C^n\) immersion that is a topological embedding (`Geometry/Manifold/SmoothEmbedding.lean`).

Smooth bump functions exist and their supports form a neighbourhood basis on a \(C^\infty\) manifold (`Geometry/Manifold/BumpFunction.lean`). On a Hausdorff \(\sigma\)-compact manifold modelled on a finite-dimensional real space, closed sets admit locally finite bump coverings subordinate to any neighbourhood assignment (`SmoothBumpCovering.exists_isSubordinate`), and \(C^\infty\) manifolds admit smooth partitions of unity and the smooth Urysohn lemma (`Geometry/Manifold/PartitionOfUnity.lean`). Continuous maps into a normed space are approximated by \(C^n\) maps within a continuous positive tolerance (`Geometry/Manifold/SmoothApprox.lean`). A compact Hausdorff \(C^\infty\) manifold modelled on a finite-dimensional real space admits a \(C^\infty\) closed embedding with injective differential into some \(\mathbb{R}^n\) (`exists_embedding_euclidean_of_compact`, `Geometry/Manifold/WhitneyEmbedding.lean`); \(n\) is not pinned to \(2m+1\), and the \(\sigma\)-compact embedding into \(\mathbb{R}^{2m+1}\) is a TODO. A \(\sigma\)-compact Hausdorff charted space modelled on a finite-dimensional real space is metrizable (`Manifold.metrizableSpace`, `Geometry/Manifold/Metrizable.lean`).

On a \(C^1\) real manifold modelled on a complete normed space, a vector field that is \(C^1\) at an interior point has a local integral curve there; on a Hausdorff manifold, integral curves that agree at one time agree on their common interval while they stay in the interior, and a global integral curve on a boundaryless manifold is periodic or injective (`Geometry/Manifold/IntegralCurve/ExistUnique.lean`). If one \(\varepsilon>0\) works at every point, every point lies on a global integral curve (`Geometry/Manifold/IntegralCurve/UniformTime.lean`).

### TauCeti

**Curvature.** TauCeti defines the curvature operator \(R(X,Y)Z=\nabla_X\nabla_YZ-\nabla_Y\nabla_XZ-\nabla_{[X,Y]}Z\) of a smooth connection and its pointwise tensor ([curvature](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Basic.lean)). For a torsion-free connection on the tangent bundle the first Bianchi identity \(R(X,Y)Z+R(Y,Z)X+R(Z,X)Y=0\) holds, and with metric compatibility it gives pair-interchange symmetry of the \((0,4)\) Riemann tensor and symmetry of the Ricci tensor ([Bianchi identity](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Bianchi.lean), [Riemann tensor](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Riemann.lean)). Sectional curvature, the Ricci tensor as a trace, and scalar curvature as its metric trace are defined, with constant curvature \(\kappa\) giving \(\mathop{\mathrm{Ric}}=(n-1)\kappa g\) and \(R=n(n-1)\kappa\) ([sectional curvature](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Sectional.lean), [Ricci](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Ricci.lean), [scalar curvature](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Scalar.lean)). The Schouten tensor and the trace-free Weyl tensor \(W=\mathop{\mathrm{Rm}}-P\owedge g\) are defined, and constant curvature gives \(W=0\) ([Schouten](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Schouten.lean), [Weyl](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Weyl.lean)). Smoothness of the Levi-Civita connection of a smooth metric is proved, with finite-regularity versions tracking the derivative loss ([regularity](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/LeviCivita/Regularity.lean)). A smooth Riemannian isometry carries curvature tensor, sectional, Ricci, and scalar curvature to those of the target ([curvature invariance](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Isometry/Curvature.lean)). The second Bianchi identity and \(\mathop{\mathrm{div}}\mathop{\mathrm{Ric}}=\tfrac12dR\) were not found.

**Geodesics and Hopf–Rinow.** Geodesics, the exponential map, and maximal geodesics are developed. For a finite-dimensional boundaryless Riemannian manifold whose metric is the Riemannian distance (`IsRiemannianManifold`, which makes the distance finite and so replaces a connectedness hypothesis), the following are equivalent at any point \(p\): \(\exp_p\) is defined on all of \(T_pM\), \(M\) is proper, \(M\) is complete, \(M\) is geodesically complete, and an exhaustion condition on compact sets (`TauCeti.Manifold.tfae_expDomain_eq_univ`); each implies that every point is joined to \(p\) by a minimizing geodesic segment ([Hopf–Rinow](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/HopfRinow.lean)). A compact Riemannian manifold is geodesically complete, with no connectedness hypothesis ([compact case](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Compact.lean)), and a locally compact Riemannian manifold with transitive isometry group is complete ([homogeneous case](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Homogeneous.lean)).

The Gauss lemma for the Riemannian exponential map is proved, with radial minimization and uniqueness of minimizing geodesics in normal balls ([Gauss lemma](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Gauss/Basic.lean), [minimization](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Gauss/Minimization.lean)). In normal coordinates centred at \(p\), the coordinate fields are parallel at \(p\), so the Christoffel symbols vanish there ([normal coordinates](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Normal/Coordinates.lean)).

**First variation.** For a variation \(F\) of a curve \(\gamma\) that is \(C^2\) near \(\{0\}\times[a,b]\), on a \(C^2\) manifold with a \(C^1\) metric, the energy satisfies \(\frac{d}{ds}E(F_s)|_0=\langle V,\dot\gamma\rangle|_a^b-\int_a^b\langle V,D_t\dot\gamma\rangle\,dt\) ([first variation](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/FirstVariation.lean)). On a boundaryless \(C^n\) manifold, \(2\le n\le\infty\), a curve that is \(C^n\) near \([a,b]\) is a critical point of the energy among fixed-endpoint \(C^n\) variations exactly when it is a geodesic on \((a,b)\) (`TauCeti.Manifold.isEnergyCritical_iff_isGeodesicCurveOn`, [geodesics as critical points](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/FirstVariation.lean)). The second variation, Jacobi fields, and conjugate points were not found.

**Volume.** A continuous Riemannian metric determines the Riemannian volume measure, glued from the chart measures \(\sqrt{\det g}\,dx\) without an orientation, and it is invariant under isometries ([volume](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/VolumeDensity/Volume.lean), [isometry invariance](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/VolumeDensity/Isometry.lean)). No volume comparison is proved.

**Supporting material.** In the synthetic setting of metric measure spaces, the measure-contraction property \(\mathrm{MCP}(K,N)\) with \(K,N>0\) bounds the diameter of the support of the reference measure by \(\pi\sqrt{(N-1)/K}\) (`TauCeti.MetricMeasureSpace.MeasureContraction.diam_support_le`, [measure contraction](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/MetricMeasure/MeasureContraction.lean)). This is the Bonnet–Myers diameter bound for such spaces; no Riemannian manifold with a Ricci bound is shown to satisfy \(\mathrm{MCP}\), so entry 52.7 is not obtained.

## Functional inequalities, harmonic functions, and heat

[Mathematical scope](SYNOPSIS52.md#functional-inequalities-harmonic-functions-and-heat).

### Mathlib

**Supporting material.** The divergence theorem that is proved is Euclidean: for a map on a rectangular box in \(\mathbb{R}^{n+1}\), continuous on the box, differentiable off a countable subset of the interior, and with integrable divergence, the integral of the divergence equals the signed sum of the face integrals (`MeasureTheory/Integral/DivergenceTheorem.lean`), with a Henstock–Kurzweil version in `Analysis/BoxIntegral/DivergenceTheorem.lean`. This is the divergence theorem of Section 32, not a Riemannian one.

### TauCeti

**Supporting material.** The Riemannian divergence is the trace of the Levi-Civita derivative, the Hessian is the Levi-Civita derivative of the gradient, and the Laplace–Beltrami operator is \(\Delta=\mathop{\mathrm{div}}\circ\mathop{\mathrm{grad}}\), the trace of the Hessian in an orthonormal basis; on a Euclidean space these agree with the flat operators ([Laplace–Beltrami](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Laplacian.lean)). A Riemannian divergence theorem connecting this divergence with the volume measure was not located, and no Sobolev space on a manifold, Bochner formula, gradient estimate, or heat kernel was found.

**Synopsis entries.** Not covered: 52.13–52.25, 52.28.

## Conformal geometry and critical equations

[Mathematical scope](SYNOPSIS52.md#conformal-geometry-and-critical-equations).

### TauCeti

Under a conformal change \(\hat g=fg\) with \(f>0\), \(\langle v,w\rangle_{\hat g}=f\langle v,w\rangle_g\), \(\mathop{\mathrm{grad}}_{\hat g}u=f^{-1}\mathop{\mathrm{grad}}_gu\), and \(dV_{\hat g}=f^{n/2}dV_g\) ([conformal change](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Conformal.lean)). On a compact manifold, for \(u\ge0\) with \(f=u^{4/(n-2)}\) of class \(C^n\) and positive, \(\mathop{\mathrm{Vol}}(\hat g)=\int u^{2n/(n-2)}\,dV_g\) (`TauCeti.WithMetric.riemannianTotalVolume_rescale_rpow`, [conformal volume](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/VolumeDensity/Conformal.lean)). The transformation law of scalar curvature, and so the conformal scalar-curvature equation of entry 52.29, is not proved; the Schouten and Weyl tensors are defined (see the first section).

For \(n\ne2\) and \(t>0\), the Euclidean bubbles \(U_{t,b}(x)=\bigl(2t/(t^2+\|x-b\|^2)\bigr)^{(n-2)/2}\) are smooth, positive, and solve \(a_n(-\Delta U)=n(n-1)U^{p_n-1}\) with \(a_n=4(n-1)/(n-2)\) and \(p_n=2n/(n-2)\) ([Yamabe bubbles](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Yamabe/Bubble.lean)); these are local models for the Yamabe problem, not its solution.

## Symplectic, gauge, and sub-Riemannian analysis

[Mathematical scope](SYNOPSIS52.md#symplectic-gauge-and-sub-riemannian-analysis).

### Mathlib

**Supporting material.** Differential forms are defined on normed spaces, as continuous alternating maps; \(d\) is linear, \(d^2=0\) for forms of sufficient smoothness, and the Cartan formula for \(d\omega\) on vector fields is proved there (`Analysis/Calculus/DifferentialForm/Basic.lean`, `Analysis/Calculus/DifferentialForm/VectorField.lean`); the module records that forms on manifolds are not yet defined. The bundle of continuous alternating maps between two \(C^n\) vector bundles is a \(C^n\) vector bundle (`Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/ContinuousAlternatingMap.lean`). A Lie group of class \(C^n\) has \(C^n\) multiplication and inversion, without second countability or finite dimension; normed spaces and units of complete normed algebras are examples (`Geometry/Manifold/Algebra/LieGroup.lean`). For a group that is \(C^3\) over \(\mathbb R\) or \(\mathbb C\) (analytic otherwise), the tangent space at the identity is a Lie algebra under the bracket of left-invariant vector fields (`Geometry/Manifold/GroupLieAlgebra.lean`). The spin group of a quadratic form (`LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/SpinGroup.lean`) and the Hodge star on an exterior power (`LinearAlgebra/ExteriorPower/HodgeStar.lean`) are algebraic and are not stated on a manifold.

### TauCeti

**\(J\)-holomorphic curves.** For a compatible pair \((\omega,J)\) on a vector space, the energy of a map from a plane domain, \(E(u)=\tfrac12\int|du|^2\), satisfies the integrated Wirtinger inequality \(\int u^*\omega\le E(u)\), with equality when \(du\) is complex-linear almost everywhere (`TauCeti.SymplecticForm.fderiv_stdComplexLineEnergy_eq_lintegral_symplecticForm`, [integrated energy](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/Energy/Integral.lean)); on a manifold with a smooth two-form and almost complex structure the pointwise energy–area identity and Wirtinger inequality are proved ([manifold energy](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/Energy.lean)). Zero energy forces a constant map on a connected plane domain into a tame symplectic vector space ([rigidity](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/Energy/Rigidity.lean)).

For \(J\) of class \(C^2\) on a vector space, the energy density of a \(J\)-holomorphic curve satisfies \(\Delta|\partial_su|^2\ge-A|\partial_su|^4\), giving a mean-value inequality ([mean value](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/Energy/MeanValue.lean)); curves of small energy with image in a compact set have a common Lipschitz bound on the half disc, and so a uniformly convergent subsequence, with no regularity of the limit asserted ([small-energy compactness](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/Energy/Compactness.lean)). A finite-energy solution on a punctured disc with compact image extends continuously across the puncture; differentiability there is not asserted ([removable singularity](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/RemovableSingularity.lean)). For the perturbed Cauchy–Riemann operator \(\partial_su+A(z)\partial_tu\) with \(A\) uniformly close to a constant compatible complex structure, an interior \(L^2\) estimate holds with a cutoff error term ([interior estimate](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/InteriorEstimate.lean)), and the local-coordinate linearization of the Cauchy–Riemann defect is computed without Sobolev completion or a Fredholm statement ([linearization](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/Linearization.lean)). Bubble trees and sphere compactness are absent.

**Morse theory.** Finite-dimensional Morse theory is developed on a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, in the analytic form needed for Morse homology: the Morse lemma and index; genericity, in that for almost every linear functional \(a\) the function \(f-a\) is Morse ([genericity](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Morse/Generic.lean)); existence and convergence of negative-gradient trajectories on compact sets with finitely many critical points, exponential at nondegenerate points ([convergence](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Morse/Convergence.lean)); local and global stable and unstable sets ([local to global](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Morse/LocalToGlobal.lean)), which, for a \(C^2\) function with globally Lipschitz gradient, carry \(C^1\) manifold structures of the expected dimensions ([stable manifolds](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Morse/Manifold.lean)); under transversality, level slices of connecting trajectories are \(C^1\) submanifolds of dimension \(\mathop{\mathrm{ind}}p-\mathop{\mathrm{ind}}q-1\) ([trajectory spaces](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Morse/TrajectorySpace.lean)); and trajectories accumulating at a nondegenerate critical point break there ([breaking](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Morse/Breaking.lean)). Under compact-sublevel and Morse–Smale hypotheses, counting isolated trajectories between critical points of consecutive index defines a mod-two boundary operator, and \(\partial^2=0\) is shown equivalent to the evenness of every set of broken index-two trajectories (`TauCeti.morseBoundary_comp_morseBoundary_eq_zero_iff`, [Morse boundary](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Morse/Boundary/Basic.lean), [square of the boundary](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Morse/Boundary/Square.lean)). The gluing that would give \(\partial^2=0\), the setting of a closed manifold, and the comparison with singular homology are not proved.

**Supporting material.** Differential forms on manifolds with corners, their exterior derivative, wedge product, pullback, and Lie derivative are developed, and the de Rham complex of a \(C^2\) manifold with corners is constructed, with cohomology presented as closed modulo exact forms ([de Rham complex](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Basic.lean)); smooth two-forms and their chart-independent closedness are a separate layer used by the symplectic files ([closed two-forms](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/TwoForm/Closed.lean)). Stokes’ theorem is proved: on a Hausdorff \(\sigma\)-compact smooth oriented \((n+1)\)-manifold modelled on the half-space, for a compactly supported \(C^1\) \(n\)-form \(\varphi\), \(\int_Md\varphi=\int_{\partial M}\varphi\) with the outward-first boundary orientation ([Stokes](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Integration/Stokes.lean)); for any model with corners, \(\int_Md\varphi=0\) when the support of \(\varphi\) is compact and disjoint from the boundary ([Stokes away from the boundary](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Integration/BoundarylessStokes.lean)). For finite-dimensional smooth real Lie groups without boundary, the identification of the tangent space at the identity with left-invariant derivations preserves the bracket ([Lie-algebra equivalence](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/Tangent/LieEquiv.lean)), and the Lie-group exponential has a smooth local inverse near zero ([local logarithm](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/5cdecf40e99d5a5ad0833afff31f5b007d68a149/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/Exponential/LocalInverse.lean)). Connections on principal bundles, Yang–Mills theory, Seiberg–Witten theory, globally hyperbolic Lorentzian manifolds, and sub-Riemannian geometry were not found.

## What is present nearby

The power-series exponential of a complete normed algebra over \(\mathbb{R}\) or \(\mathbb{C}\) satisfies \(\exp((s+t)a)=\exp(sa)\exp(ta)\), because \(sa\) and \(ta\) commute (`NormedSpace.exp_add_of_commute`). The orbit \(t\mapsto\exp(ta)\) is therefore a norm-continuous one-parameter group inside the group of units (`Analysis/Normed/Algebra/Exponential.lean`). Section 20 states the exponential. This identity is not a generation theorem for strongly continuous semigroups.

The continuous functional calculus of a normal or self-adjoint element of a C⋆-algebra is the star-isometric equivalence of sections 21 and 23, from continuous functions on the spectrum onto the closed star-subalgebra generated by the element (`Analysis/CStarAlgebra/ContinuousFunctionalCalculus/Basic.lean`).

For a positive element \(a\) of a C⋆-algebra and \(0<p<1\), there is a measure \(\mu\) on \(\mathbb R\) with \(a^p=\int t^p\bigl(t^{-1}-(t+a)^{-1}\bigr)\,d\mu(t)\), with the integral taken in the continuous functional calculus; an analogous representation holds for \(1<p<2\) (`CFC.exists_measure_nnrpow_eq_integral_cfcₙ_rpowIntegrand₀₁` and `…₁₂` in `Analysis/SpecialFunctions/ContinuousFunctionalCalculus/Rpow/IntegralRepresentation.lean`). The measure is determined only up to a constant factor; the file uses the representation to prove operator monotonicity and concavity of \(a\mapsto a^p\) for \(0\le p\le1\) (`Rpow/Order.lean`). This is the C⋆-algebraic shadow of the Balakrishnan formula, not a fractional-power theory for sectorial operators on Banach spaces.

Ordinary differential equations, including the Picard–Lindelöf theorem, are section 31 (`Analysis/ODE/PicardLindelof.lean`). Algebraic semigroups lie outside this note.

## Additional coverage in TauCeti

The following additions were checked in TauCeti revision `6ebb29f84b4d`; see the [revision, build evidence, and comparison scope](TauCetiCoverage.md). Source links below are pinned to that revision.

TauCeti contains a developed theory of strongly continuous semigroups on real Banach spaces. It defines their partially defined generators and proves density of the generator domain, of every iterated domain \(D(A^n)\) and of the smooth vectors ([smooth vectors](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generator/SmoothDensity.lean)), closedness of the generator, orbit differentiation, exponential growth bounds \(\|S(t)\|\le Me^{\omega t}\), and resolvent estimates. The Hille–Yosida theorem is an actual generation equivalence: for a real Banach space, growth bound \(M e^{\omega t}\) is characterized by dense domain, resolvent existence for every real \(\lambda>\omega\), and the bounds \(\|R(\lambda,A)^n\|\le M/(\lambda-\omega)^n\), with \(M\ge1\) ([Hille–Yosida](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generation/HilleYosida/Generation.lean)). Dense maximal dissipativity characterizes generators of contraction semigroups, in the Lumer–Phillips theorem ([source](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generation/LumerPhillips.lean)).

Semigroup orbits are unique mild solutions of \(u'=Au\), and unique classical solutions for initial data in the generator domain ([solutions](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/CauchyProblem/Basic.lean), [uniqueness](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/CauchyProblem/Uniqueness.lean)); only the homogeneous problem is treated. Bounded perturbations \(A+B\) generate semigroups on the same domain, with the corresponding exponential growth estimate ([perturbation](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generation/BoundedPerturbation.lean)). Further standard results are present: the generator determines the semigroup ([uniqueness](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generator/Uniqueness.lean)); a bounded operator \(A\) generates \(e^{tA}\), and a semigroup is continuous in operator norm exactly when its generator is bounded ([bounded generators](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/BoundedGenerator/Basic.lean), [uniformly continuous semigroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/UniformlyContinuous.lean)); for \(\lambda\) beyond the growth exponent the Laplace transform \(\int_0^\infty e^{-\lambda t}S(t)\,dt\) is the resolvent of the generator, with the sharp power bounds used in Hille–Yosida ([resolvent identity](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Resolvent/Identity.lean), [power bounds](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Resolvent/PowerBounds.lean)); for complex-linear semigroups the open half-plane \(\operatorname{Re}\lambda>\omega\) lies in the complex resolvent set, the resolvent is the Laplace transform there with \(\|R(\lambda,A)\|\le M/(\operatorname{Re}\lambda-\omega)\), and it is holomorphic ([complex resolvent](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Resolvent/Complex.lean)); and the Yosida approximations converge to the semigroup, uniformly on compact time intervals ([Yosida approximation](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generation/Yosida/Basic.lean)).

Strongly continuous groups, dissipativity through the duality map and the Hilbert-space criterion \(\langle Ax,x\rangle\le0\), and m-dissipativity of skew multiples \(cA\) (\(c\in i\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}\)) of self-adjoint operators are developed alongside ([groups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Group/Basic.lean), [dissipative operators](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Dissipative/Duality.lean), [Hilbert criterion](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Dissipative/Hilbert.lean), [skew multiples](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Dissipative/SkewSelfAdjoint.lean)). Stone's theorem is proved in both directions: the complex generator of a unitary group is skew-adjoint, and a self-adjoint \(A\) is \(-i\) times the complex generator of exactly one unitary group \(e^{itA}\) ([generator direction](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Group/Unitary.lean), [converse](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Group/Stone/Unbounded.lean)); see also Section 22.

Two concrete families are treated. For a nonnegative a.e. measurable multiplier \(m\) on an arbitrary measure space and \(1\le p<\infty\), \(S(t)f=e^{-tm}f\) is a contraction semigroup on \(L^p(\mu;E)\) whose generator is multiplication by \(-m\) on the exact domain \(\{f: mf\in L^p\}\), with resolvent multiplication by \((\lambda+m)^{-1}\) ([multiplication semigroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Multiplication/MeasureLp/Generator.lean)). On a finite-dimensional real inner product space and \(1\le p<\infty\), convolution with the Gaussian heat kernel is a strongly continuous contraction semigroup \(e^{t\Delta}\) on \(L^p\) with Banach-space values, and on \(C^2\) functions with compact support its generator is the Laplacian; the full generator domain is not determined ([heat semigroup](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/HeatKernel/Semigroup.lean), [generator](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/HeatKernel/Generator.lean)). This covers the linear autonomous theory of \(C_0\)-semigroups, but not sectorial-operator or holomorphic-semigroup theory.

## Topics of Section 24 not found in either inspected library

- Sectorial operators and analytic (holomorphic) semigroups, including the analytic generation theorem; fractional powers of sectorial operators and the Balakrishnan formula (Mathlib's integral representation of \(a^p\) concerns positive elements of C⋆-algebras only).
- The Trotter–Kato approximation theorem and the Chernoff product formula for strongly continuous semigroups. TauCeti proves only convergence of Yosida approximants, and its Trotter formula ([Lie groups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/Exponential/Trotter.lean)) concerns exponential curves in finite-dimensional Lie groups, not unbounded generators.
- The inhomogeneous Cauchy problem \(u'=Au+f\) and the variation-of-constants formula for a semigroup.
- Spectral mapping theorems and stability theory of semigroups (Gearhart–Prüss, Arendt–Batty–Lyubich–Vũ); TauCeti has growth bounds but no spectral bound or stability theorem.
- A holomorphic (Riesz–Dunford or \(H^\infty\)) functional calculus beyond the continuous functional calculus of sections 21 and 23; R-sectoriality and maximal regularity.
- Nonautonomous evolution equations, nonlinear (accretive) semigroups and the Crandall–Liggett theorem, and Volterra evolution equations. TauCeti's dissipativity theory is linear only.

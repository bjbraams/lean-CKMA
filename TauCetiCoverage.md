The TauCeti supplements in `Mathlib04.md` through `Mathlib53.md` were checked on 2026-10-18 against actual Lean library sources in [TauCeti revision `6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526`](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/tree/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526). Their links point to files at that revision, rather than to a moving branch or a roadmap. The same revision underlies the fresh search recorded in [GAPS-REVIEW.md](GAPS-REVIEW.md).

The comparison is with Mathlib commit `f039e1b8a616d45782a5c7fc5e7909249c36b8ea`, dated 2026-10-18, using Lean `v4.36.0-rc1`. This is newer than the Mathlib pinned in this project's `lake-manifest.json`. TauCeti uses Lean `v4.36.0-rc1` and Mathlib revision `2e318faecff7d70d4e0cf8cc875104424a737355`, as recorded in its [toolchain](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/lean-toolchain) and [dependency manifest](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/lake-manifest.json). Consequently, “additional coverage” means implemented TauCeti results beyond the **Mathlib commit inspected here**. It does not assert absence from every later Mathlib revision, nor compatibility with this project's dependencies without migration. No dependency was changed or TauCeti code installed in this project.

## Evidence of implemented results

The audit searched the library tree and inspected relevant module definitions and theorem statements, including their hypotheses and conclusions. Roadmap targets, comments describing future work, and theorem names alone were not counted as completed mathematics. For example, the Koebe square-root step is not the one-quarter theorem; an injective holomorphic area formula is not the coefficient area theorem; and a Hermite differential identity is not a self-adjointness theorem for a Schrödinger operator. Conditional constructions are described with their substantive hypotheses, such as a dual certificate for a transport map or constant principal coefficients for interior elliptic regularity.

The exact TauCeti revision had a successful public `build` status, reporting “sandboxed build passed; exact revision cache is public”, linked to [build run 39108062419](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/actions/runs/39108062419). Its [Lake configuration](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/lakefile.toml) builds the `TauCeti.*` module glob. The root `TauCeti.lean` re-exports nothing; membership in a root aggregate import is therefore not the criterion for built library content.

The repository's [build script](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/scripts/sandbox-build.sh) runs `lake build --iofail`, then audits compiled declarations. The [axiom audit](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/scripts/Axioms.lean) permits only Lean's standard `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`; it rejects `sorry`, additional axioms, and `native_decide` dependencies. This is public CI evidence, not a fresh local rebuild by this audit. No claim of independent mathematical review of every proof is intended.

## Reading the notes

The main body of each section note remains a survey of the pinned Mathlib checkout. Its “Additional coverage in TauCeti” subsection records implemented additions, sometimes including a useful specialization or packaging of Mathlib results, identified as such. Existing Mathlib theorems imported by TauCeti are not attributed to TauCeti: for example, Sion's minimax theorem was found in the local Mathlib checkout and added to the Mathlib portions of Sections 12 and 36.

The final “Topics … not found in either inspected library” list considers both source revisions. An old omission is removed when the described result is present, or narrowed when only a restricted case was found. Absence statements report the result of this source search; they are not claims about all possible deductions from the libraries or future work. Sections with no direct additional theory say so, and distinguish neighbouring prerequisites from the subject itself.

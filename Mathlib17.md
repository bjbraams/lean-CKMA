## Bases

Classical Schauder bases and unconditional Schauder bases are constructed in Section 15. A basis consists of vectors and continuous coordinate functionals, biorthogonal, with every vector equal to the corresponding sum. On a Banach space the finite-rank projections attached to the basis are uniformly bounded, and the supremum of their norms is recorded as the basis constant (`enormProjBound`, `nnnormProjBound`). Nested finite-rank projections converging pointwise to the identity, with rank-one successive differences, produce a classical Schauder basis. A Hilbert basis is an unconditional Schauder basis, and a Hilbert basis indexed by \(\mathbb{N}\) is a classical one (`Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases.lean`, `Analysis/InnerProductSpace/l2Space.lean`).

## A neighbouring convexity statement

A real seminormed group is uniformly convex when, for every \(\varepsilon > 0\), some \(\delta > 0\) forces \(\|x+y\| \le 2-\delta\) whenever \(x\) and \(y\) are unit vectors at least \(\varepsilon\) apart. A real inner product space is uniformly convex. The file lists the Milman–Pettis theorem and Hanner’s inequalities as future work; TauCeti proves Milman–Pettis (Section 15). The development belongs with Section 12 (`Analysis/Convex/Uniform.lean`, `Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Convex.lean`).

Compact operators and Fredholm operators, including the index and its local constancy, are Sections 15 and 21.

The algebraic tensor product of a finite family of normed spaces carries a projective seminorm, with the isometric universal property for continuous multilinear maps and norm bounds for tensor products of operators (`Analysis/Normed/Module/PiTensorProduct/ProjectiveSeminorm.lean`). Section 16 gives its scope and limitations. It does not provide a theory of nuclear or summing operators.

## Complemented subspaces, L-projections, and isometries

A closed subspace of a Banach space is complemented when it is the range of a continuous linear projection. In a Banach space, algebraic complements that are both closed are topological complements, by the open mapping theorem; finite-dimensional subspaces and closed subspaces of finite codimension are complemented (`Analysis/Normed/Module/Complemented.lean`, `Topology/Algebra/Module/FiniteDimension.lean`). Sobczyk's and Pełczyński's theorems on complementation in \(c_0\) and \(\ell^p\) were not found.

An L-projection on a normed space is a projection \(P\) with \(\|x\|=\|Px\|+\|(1-P)x\|\), and an M-projection one with \(\|x\|=\max(\|Px\|,\|(1-P)x\|)\). The L-projections commute and form a Boolean algebra (`Analysis/Normed/Module/MStructure.lean`). The Boolean algebra of M-projections, completeness of the L-projection algebra, and M-ideals are listed there as motivation, not proved.

The Mazur–Ulam theorem, the starting point of the nonlinear classification theory of Benyamini–Lindenstrauss: a surjective isometry between real normed spaces is affine (`Analysis/Normed/Affine/MazurUlam.lean`). Lipschitz and uniform classification of Banach spaces beyond this were not found.

## Additional coverage in TauCeti

The following additions were checked in TauCeti revision `6ebb29f84b4d`; see the [revision, build evidence, and comparison scope](TauCetiCoverage.md). Source links below are pinned to that revision.

**Bases.** Building on Mathlib's `GeneralSchauderBasis`, TauCeti proves that every strictly increasing subsequence of a Schauder basis is a basis of its closed span, with basis constant at most the original one ([subsequences](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/Subsequence.lean)); that bounded linear equivalences transport bases ([transport](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/Map.lean)); that the norm \(\sup_n\|P_nx\|\) is an equivalent norm in which the basis is monotone ([monotone renorming](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/Monotone.lean)); and the small-perturbation principle: if \(\delta=\sum_i\|e_i^*\|\,\|e_i-f_i\|<1\) in a Banach space, a linear isomorphism with norm at most \(1+\delta\) and inverse norm at most \((1-\delta)^{-1}\) sends \(e_i\) to \(f_i\), so \((f_i)\) is a basis ([perturbation](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/Perturbation.lean)). The coordinate functionals form a basis of their closed span in the dual, and they form a basis of the whole dual exactly when the basis is shrinking ([dual basic sequence](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/Dual.lean)). **James's basis theorem** holds: a Banach space with a Schauder basis is reflexive if and only if the basis is shrinking and boundedly complete (`surjective_inclusionInDoubleDual_iff`, [reflexivity](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/Reflexive.lean)).

**Approximation properties.** The approximation property, the bounded approximation property with constant \(C\), and the metric approximation property are defined by finite-rank operators approximating the identity uniformly on compact sets; a uniformly bounded net of finite-rank operators converging pointwise to the identity gives the BAP ([definitions](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/ApproximationProperty/Basic.lean)). The properties pass to bounded retracts and are invariant under isomorphism, with MAP preserved by isometries and contractive projections ([transport](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/ApproximationProperty/Transport.lean)). A Banach space with a Schauder basis, or with an unconditional basis, has the BAP with the basis constant as bound, and the MAP when the projections are contractions ([bases](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/Approximation.lean)). Enflo's counterexample is not formalized.

**Approximation numbers and operator ideals.** For \(T\colon E\to F\) between seminormed spaces, the approximation numbers \(a_n(T)=\inf\{\|T-R\|:\operatorname{rank}R\le n\}\) satisfy \(a_{m+n}(S+T)\le a_m(S)+a_n(T)\), \(a_{m+n}(ST)\le a_m(S)a_n(T)\), and \(a_n(ATB)\le\|A\|a_n(T)\|B\|\) ([approximation numbers](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/ApproximationNumber/Basic.lean)). Over a complete field \(a_n(T)=0\) exactly when \(\operatorname{rank}T\le n\); \(a_n(T)\to0\) implies compactness for a complete target over a locally compact field, and conversely for compact operators into inner product spaces ([compactness](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/ApproximationNumber/Compact.lean)). Between Hilbert spaces, \(a_n(T^*)=a_n(T)\), and on finite-dimensional spaces the approximation numbers are the singular values (Eckart–Young). A symmetric gauge on sequences (a symmetric norming function) induces an operator-ideal family on Hilbert spaces with the triangle and two-sided ideal inequalities; the normed ideal of finite gauge is packaged as a normed space ([families](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/Family.lean), [symmetric gauges](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/Family/SymmetricGauge.lean), [carriers](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/Carrier.lean)). The Ky Fan families are complete. The nuclear gauge \(\sum_na_n(T)\) is subadditive, adjoint invariant and lower semicontinuous, and finite gauge forces compactness ([nuclear gauge](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/Nuclear/Basic.lean)); the Hilbert–Schmidt energy \(\sum_i\|Te_i\|^2\) equals \(\sum_na_n(T)^2\) ([Hilbert–Schmidt](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/ApproximationNumber/HilbertSchmidt.lean)). Schatten \(p\)-seminorms are constructed for maps between finite-dimensional inner product spaces ([Schatten seminorms](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm/Schatten.lean)). These are \(s\)-number ideals on Hilbert spaces; nuclear operators between Banach spaces as sums \(\sum x_n^*\otimes y_n\), \(p\)-summing operators, and Pietsch factorization are not formalized.

TauCeti also proves part of compact-operator and Fredholm theory useful alongside operator ideals. For a compact operator \(K\) on a Banach space, \(I-K\) has finite-dimensional kernel, closed range, and finite-dimensional cokernel ([Riesz theory](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Compact/RieszTheory.lean)). Compact perturbations preserve the Fredholm index ([source](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/6ebb29f84b4d6e2019233da0718776a6e32e1526/TauCeti/Analysis/Fredholm/CompactPerturbation.lean)). Mazur's lemma and the Milman–Pettis theorem are recorded in Section 15. Type and cotype were not located.

## Topics of Section 17 not found in either inspected library

- Rademacher type and cotype, Khintchine's and Kahane's inequalities, Kwapień's theorem, and Grothendieck's inequality.
- Banach–Mazur distance, John's ellipsoid theorem, Dvoretzky's theorem, and the local theory of finite-dimensional normed spaces.
- Weak-sequence structure: Rosenthal's \(\ell^1\) theorem and the Dunford–Pettis characterization of weak compactness in \(L^1\).
- The Radon–Nikodým property, smoothness, superreflexivity and renorming theory, and the classical structure theory of \(c_0\), \(\ell^p\), and \(L^p\) (Pitt's theorem, Pełczyński's decomposition method, Sobczyk's theorem).
- Enflo's example of a space without the approximation property. TauCeti defines the approximation, bounded approximation, and metric approximation properties and proves the BAP for spaces with a basis.
- Schatten classes on infinite-dimensional Hilbert spaces as concrete \(\ell^p\) ideals for \(p\ne2\). TauCeti has the general symmetric-gauge ideal families, the nuclear gauge, the Hilbert–Schmidt identification, and finite-dimensional Schatten seminorms, but no \(\ell^p\) gauge instance on infinite sequences.
- Nuclear operators between Banach spaces, absolutely summing operators, and Pietsch's domination theorem.
- A geometry of bases beyond the topics above: unconditional basic sequences in general Banach spaces, block bases, and the Bessaga–Pełczyński selection principle.
